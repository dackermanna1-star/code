Checked **Oct 31, 2026** against each property's own website or leasing
widget unless marked *unverified*. Units and prices change daily, so call
before applying.

**What's included:** true 1-bedrooms, market rate (no income limits), in
elevator mid-rise or high-rise buildings, base rent strictly under $2,180.
"All-in" means base rent plus the **mandatory** monthly fees the property
publishes. Usage-based water, sewer and electric are not included.

in a modern non-garden building. Every one that qualifies is in **20171**:
the Dulles Station / Innovation Center Metro / McNair Farms area of Herndon.

## Ask every leasing office
1. "What is the **total monthly cost** for unit ___ on a **12-month** lease,
   including all required fees?"
2. "Is this unit market rate or WDU/affordable?"
3. "Is the current special still valid, and is the quoted price before or
   after the special?"
4. If using a housing voucher: "Do you accept vouchers, and what are the
   average monthly utility costs?" (Voucher limits usually count rent plus
   utilities.)
