# Textures: PBR sets, vegetation cut-outs, decals and noise

There are 38 tileable PBR sets (11 terrain, 24 building/prop, 3 bark), 4 alpha-cut vegetation atlases, 5 decals and
a blue-noise texture. Together they take about 64 MB. The HDRIs are in `../hdri/` (about 14 MB, see its README).
Everything is **CC0**: Poly Haven, ambientCG, TextureCan, or generated by `tools/assets/textures/generate.py`.
`manifest.json` lists every file with its pixel size, byte size, real-world tile size, source URL and license.

## Conventions (all sets)

| file | content | three.js |
|---|---|---|
| `albedo.jpg` | base colour, sRGB | `map`, `colorSpace = SRGBColorSpace` |
| `normal.jpg` | tangent-space **OpenGL** normal (green = +V) | `normalMap`, `NoColorSpace` (do **not** flip Y) |
| `arm.jpg` | R = AO, G = roughness, B = metalness (linear) | same texture on `aoMap`, `roughnessMap` and `metalnessMap`, with `roughness = 1`, `metalness = 1` |
| `height.jpg` | linear 0..1, normalized to the 0.5–99.5 % range (terrain sets and tire tracks) | height-blend between terrain layers, or parallax |

* All sets are seamless. Every resize wrap-pads the source so the edges still match, and `build.py` measures the seam
  for each output. Set `wrapS = wrapT = RepeatWrapping` and an anisotropy of 8 or more (terrain and floors are seen at
  grazing angles).
* `tile_m` in the manifest is the real-world size of one UV repeat in metres, `[u, v]`. Repeat = surface size ÷
  `tile_m`. The value comes from Poly Haven `dimensions` or ambientCG `dimensionX`. `tile_estimated: true` means the
  source had no dimensions and the value was estimated from feature sizes.
* `Assets.pbr(setId, {repeat})` in `src/core/Assets.ts` already loads this layout.
* **Terrain sets at 2048 px** ship albedo and normal at 2048 but `arm.jpg` and `height.jpg` at 1024. AO, roughness and
  height are low-frequency, and this saves about 40 %. The 2048 normals use 4:2:0 JPEG chroma. The x/y detail survives
  in luma, the files are half the size, and no artifacts are visible in the renders. The 1024 sets use full 4:4:4
  chroma for normals.

**Repetition at distance.** In an eye-height render over a 60 m patch (`contact.mjs --groups ground`), the grid shows on
`terrain_grass_dry`, `terrain_forest_floor`, `terrain_dirt` and `terrain_mud` beyond about 15 m. The terrain shader
should break it up with one or more of the following:
1. A second sample at about 0.27× scale blended by distance.
2. A low-frequency world-space noise that modulates albedo brightness and saturation by ±10–15 %.
3. Stochastic or hex tiling for grass and dirt.
4. Height-based layer blending using `height.jpg`.

## Vegetation cut-outs (`vegetation` in the manifest)

Files are `albedo.jpg` + `opacity.jpg` + `normal.jpg` + `arm.jpg`, 1024², from ambientCG atlases. Colour has been
dilated outward from the opaque texels, so mips and bilinear filtering do not produce dark fringes. Suggested material:
`map: albedo, alphaMap: opacity, alphaTest: 0.5, side: DoubleSide`. With MSAA on, `alphaToCoverage: true` keeps thin
blades from vanishing in the distance. For grass cards, also bend the normals toward up.

* `veg_grass_blades` (Foliage001): 10 individual blades. The base of each blade is at the image bottom (v = 0).
* `veg_grass_seedheads` (Foliage002): 4 wild-grass stems with seed heads, base at the bottom.
* `veg_leaves_broadleaf` (LeafSet024): 3 × 3 green beech-like leaves (2 are insect-eaten).
* `veg_pine_needles` (PineNeedles001): 2 × 5 needle clusters lying horizontally. The scan is of dry brown needles, so
  the albedo was **recoloured to living green**, keeping its luminance detail.

## Noise

`noise/blue_noise_64.png` is 8-bit greyscale. `noise/blue_noise_64_rgba.png` holds 4 independent channels. Both are
void-and-cluster blue noise (σ 1.9) and tile seamlessly. Its low-frequency energy is 0.8 % of the high-frequency energy.
Use `NearestFilter`, `RepeatWrapping`, `NoColorSpace`, and offset per frame (for example R2 sequence × 64) for temporal
dithering: shadow PCF rotation, SSAO and SSR jitter, alpha-to-coverage dithering, LOD cross-fades.

## Quality notes and gaps

* **Estimated tile sizes:** `terrain_rock`, `plaster_white`, `wall_tiles`, `fabric`, `bark_birch`, `bark_oak`. Adjust if
  they look off in game.
* **Generated content:**
  * Bullet holes and cracks are procedural. They hold up at gameplay distances but are not hero close-up quality.
  * The camo print and wallpaper print are generated, but the weave and paper detail beneath them are photo-scanned.
* **Not found as CC0, so not shipped:** a European birch-leaf atlas (the broadleaf atlas is beech-like), a crop or wheat
  atlas, and a plowed-field scan without a border (the furrow set was repaired as described above).
* `container_metal` is green only. Ask if red/blue variants are needed; a desaturated copy costs about 0.2 MB.
* Roughness was remapped on `terrain_grass`, `terrain_dirt`, `terrain_rock`, `terrain_sand` and `terrain_gravel`. The
  scans' values (0.26–0.55) made dry ground look wet under the sun.

## Rebuild / preview

```
tools/assets/textures/build_all.sh <scratch_dir>       # downloads into <scratch_dir>/src, rebuilds everything here + ../hdri
node tools/assets/textures/static_server.mjs --port 5261 &
node tools/assets/textures/contact.mjs <outDir> --port 5261 --groups terrain,ground,building,bark,veg,decal,hdri
```
