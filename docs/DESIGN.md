# HALLOWELL — *the things left behind*

> Design bible. Source of truth for story, characters, rules, chapters, maps, puzzles and endings.
> Engineering contract lives in `docs/TECH.md`. If the two disagree on *what happens*, this file wins;
> on *how it is coded*, TECH.md wins.

---

## 0. Pillars (read these first, every agent)

1. **The building is the monster's body of rules.** Every horror in the game obeys a school rule
   (bells, seats, hall passes, attendance, no running). Players survive by *learning the rules*, not by
   reflexes. Nothing kills the player arbitrarily.
2. **Restraint.** Long quiet stretches. Silence before events. The Colonel is shown in fragments; each
   appearance reveals one new thing. No loud stinger without earned tension.
3. **Caring is the horror.** The friends are funny, specific and real before they vanish. Their absence
   is the soundtrack of the middle game. The ending is decided by how Tess treated them.
4. **Spatial truth.** The school is a real 1923 Pennsylvania K–12 building. Players should be able to draw
   it from memory. Rooms change on revisits; the *layout* never lies — except where the school is
   deliberately lying (front doors, the long hall), and those lies are rules too.
5. **Nostalgic, not derivative.** 1996 detail is texture, never a punchline. Teen dialogue is
   understated: interruptions, half-jokes, evasions. No forced slang, no exposition dumps.

## 1. Title, tone, frame

- **Title:** HALLOWELL. Subtitle on the title screen: *the things left behind*.
- **Kids' nickname for the school:** "Hollow Hell."
- **Setting:** Kettle Run, Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania (fictional town in the anthracite coal region).
  Friday night **October 18, 1996**, ~9:40 PM, through dawn of **Saturday October 19** — Founder's Day.
- **Look:** RPG-Maker-style 3/4 top-down pixel art at 480×270, 16 px tiles, VN-style portraits, strong
  darkness with a flashlight cone, dust in beams, rain on windows, buzzing fluorescents that only come on
  when the school "wakes."
- **Palette:** dirty institutional green, nicotine cream, faded mustard, cold moonlight blue, rust brown,
  wet black. Unnatural accents only for the supernatural: the Colonel's crimson sash and brass, the amber
  of husks, the red of the mine fire, the orange of a safety-patrol belt.
- **Playtime target:** ~60–80 min first run. Four endings.

## 2. The truth (what really happened)

Players piece this together; never state it all in one place except the final documents.

- **Colonel Cyrus Hallowell (1841–1911).** Commanded a regiment of Schuylkill County miners in the Civil
  War who dug the mine beneath Petersburg (1864); he watched the earth swallow men and came home a hero.
  Founded **Hallowell Coal & Iron Co.** (1866), built the Kettle Run colliery and the great **Hallowell
  Breaker**. Owned the town: houses, store, church. Legendary appetite; grew immense. Children of miners
  (8–14) worked his breaker as **breaker boys**, picking slate from coal. He called them "my boys," knew
  every name, and kept a personal ledger of every boy on his payroll — the first **Register**.
- **October 19, 1894 — the Breaker fire.** Seventeen breaker boys died in the chutes. The Colonel was in
  the weigh-master's office at the top and walked out unhurt. The inquiry blamed "carelessness with a
  lamp." The breaker was never rebuilt; it was buried under culm and slag with not all the boys recovered.
- **1911.** The Colonel dies on October 19 "of a surfeit" at his Founder's Day banquet. His will — the
  **Founder's Charter** — endows a school "upon the ground of my old Breaker, that the children of Kettle Run
  shall never want for a place, and none shall be left behind," orders his entombment in a vault beneath it,
  and enrolls **himself** as its first pupil:
  > "I shall be the first scholar of Hallowell School, and subject to its rules as any child; and I shall
  > be the last to leave it."
- **1923.** Hallowell Consolidated School (K–12) opens on the buried breaker. Motto carved over the door:
  **NEMO RELINQUATUR** — *Let no one be left behind.* Founder's Day (Oct 19) = Homecoming.
- **The cycle.** Like the seventeen-year cicadas (locals still say "seventeen-year locusts"), the Colonel
  sleeps underground and wakes every 17 years on Founder's Day: **1894, 1911, 1928, 1945, 1962, 1979, 1996.**
  (These are real Brood II emergence years.) Each waking, the school "holds session": bells nobody rings,
  roll calls, and children taken into the Register. Explained away as runaways, a strip-pit drowning, "went
  to work in Philadelphia." The 1962 waking coincided with the start of the underground mine fire.
- **Walter "Walt" Pruszynski,** custodian 1958–1979, survived 1962 and worked out the rules. He kept a log.
- **October 19, 1979 — the Homecoming Assembly, 1:45 PM.** Music teacher **Miss Ruth Adair** (34) had pieced it
  together from Walt and the records. She took the principal's stamps from his safe meaning to strike names
  from the Register under the stage. Principal **Gerald Haupt** had been "signing them in" (the Colonel
  promised him the school would never close). The power failed mid-assembly. When it returned, **nine
  students and Miss Adair** were gone. Official story: carbon monoxide from the mine fire; the missing
  presumed lost in old workings. School condemned and closed. Haupt resigned. Walt quit; died 1988.
- **1984.** Federal buyout; most of Kettle Run relocated. Steam still rises from cracks in Route 61.
- **1996.** Brood II came up in May; their empty shells still cling to the oaks. Tonight is the next waking.

## 3. Characters

### Theresa "Tess" Dolan — protagonist (16)
- **Look (must read at 24×32 px):** copper-red hair, shoulder length, messy, usually tucked behind her left
  ear with loose strands in her face; freckles; pale; gray-green eyes. **Oversized, worn, light-wash denim
  jacket** (her dad's; sleeves pushed up), black T-shirt underneath, **black jeans**, **black canvas high-top
  sneakers with white side stripe and white soles** (Vans Sk8-Hi style). Olive canvas shoulder bag. Black
  Maglite-style flashlight. Posture: slightly hunched, hands in pockets when idle, flashlight held low.
- **Personality:** observant, dry, deflects with sarcasm, avoids hard conversations, draws in a sketchbook,
  loyal, braver than she believes. Not a hero; not helpless. She gets things wrong sometimes and says so.
- **Secret:** she and her mom (Maureen, an ER nurse) move to **Philadelphia** over Christmas break. In her bag:
  a **Pennsylvania Student Withdrawal/Transfer Form**, already signed by her mom, that she was supposed to
  hand in Monday. She has not told anyone. Her parents are divorced; dad lives in Pittsburgh.
- **Names:** friends call her "Tess" (Danny: "Dolan"; Leah: "T"). **Nobody calls her Theresa** — except her
  mom when she's grounded, and *the school*. She says this out loud once in the prologue.
- **Bag contents (starting inventory):** Maglite (flashlight), sketchbook (map + notes UI), her mom's
  **compact mirror** (cracked; usable after Ch5), the **transfer form**, **Leah's mixtape** ("FOR THE DRIVE"),
  a pack of Big Red gum, $4.35, house key.
- **Voice samples:**
  - "Great. A haunted school. And I wore the good sneakers."
  - "That's not a rat. Rats don't sigh."
  - (to herself) "Okay. Okay okay okay. Walk. Don't run."
  - "I'm not leaving without them. — I'm *not.*"

### Danny Kovach (16) — the instigator
- **Look:** green/silver Philadelphia Eagles Starter jacket, backwards red cap, ripped jeans, big white
  sneakers, backpack. Stocky. Always moving.
- **Personality:** class clown with a big heart; fills every silence; impressions (Mulder from *The X-Files*:
  "The truth is out there, Scully"). Dad was laid off from the strip mine and drinks; Danny is terrified
  he'll never get out of the coal region. Calls Tess **"Dolan."** Uses "youse," "heyna," "dude" lightly.
- **Why he's here:** organized the dare; his uncle Frank swears the Colonel was buried "with his medals and a
  gold sword" under the stage. Brought his uncle's **bolt cutters**, a bag of Herr's chips, Tastykakes.
- **The joke that lands wrong (prologue):** "Nine kids walk into an assembly. Zero walk out. It's like the
  worst math problem ever." (Leah: "Danny." Marcus says nothing.)
- **Thing left behind:** his cheap **red flashlight** (masking tape label "DANNY K.") — rolling on the floor,
  still on, when he vanishes. His **backpack** (with bolt cutters) stays in the lobby.
- **Voice:** "Dolan. Dolan. Look at this guy. He ate the Civil War." / "I'm the guy who gets stuck. That's my
  whole thing."

### Leah Brandt (16) — best friend since second grade
- **Look:** dark chin-length bob, round wire glasses, oversized gray cardigan, plaid skirt over black tights,
  **cherry-red Doc Martens**. Carries her dad's **VHS-C camcorder** (red record LED).
- **Personality:** sharp, skeptical, ambitious, deadpan. Writes and photocopies a zine called *STATIC*.
  Narrates into the camcorder in a mock-documentary voice. Corrects people's facts. Calls Tess **"T."**
- **Secret:** she **already knows** Tess is moving (Tess's mom told Leah's mom in August). She made Tess a
  mixtape labeled **"FOR THE DRIVE"** — she means the drive to Philadelphia. She has been waiting for Tess to
  tell her herself. Her older sister left for college and never calls; being left behind is her fear.
- **Prologue camcorder intro (establishes everyone's full names — players need these):**
  > "This is Leah Brandt for STATIC. It is nine forty-seven P.M., Friday, October eighteenth, 1996. With me
  > are Daniel Kovach—" "Danny." "—Daniel Kovach, Marcus Webb, and Theresa Dolan." "Never call me Theresa on
  > tape." "Noted. For the tape."
- **Thing left behind:** the **camcorder**, still recording on her desk in Room 103.

### Marcus Webb (16) — the quiet one
- **Look:** tall, close-cropped hair, dark-skinned, olive army-surplus field jacket, black knit beanie,
  headphones around his neck (Discman in pocket). Plays trumpet in the Mount Tabor band.
- **Personality:** few words, deliberate, kind; when he jokes it lands hard. Hums when nervous — the old
  Hallowell alma mater, which his grandmother sang. Calls Tess "Tess."
- **Secret:** his aunt **Denise Webb** was one of the Homecoming Nine. He was born in December 1979, two
  months after she vanished; his grandmother **Ivy Webb** says he has Denise's eyes, kept Denise's room the
  same for seventeen years, and still sets her a plate at Thanksgiving (Ivy is now in a nursing home in
  Pottsville). Marcus came on the anniversary to see the place. He carries Ivy's **photo of Denise** in her
  band uniform. He hasn't told the others why he came.
- **Thing left behind:** the **photo of Denise**, on the gym floor after he's taken.

### The Colonel ("the Benefactor"; Walt's log calls him "Mr. H.")
- **Silhouette:** an aristocratic warlord of impossible mass. A rotted **Union colonel's frock coat**
  (dark navy wool) stretched over a vast swollen torso, brass buttons popped or sunk into folds of pale,
  blubbery flesh; **gold epaulettes** half-swallowed by the shoulders; a **crimson officer's sash** straining
  across the belly; medals pinned directly into skin. Arms too heavy, hands huge with rings sunk into
  sausage fingers. Below the waist he is a mass he drags, the boots split open. Roughly 3 tiles wide and
  3–4 tiles tall.
- **Face (the key to him):** a **small, serene, almost-human face** — the face from his portrait, gray
  mutton chops and mustache, half-lidded eyes, a polite closed smile — **set too small in the middle of a
  huge head**, like a doll's face pressed into dough. Look closer and it's slightly too smooth, the eyes
  never quite blink in sync, and when the mouth opens it opens a little too far, vertically. No fangs, no
  glowing eyes, no gore.
- **Movement:** drags himself slowly; can stay perfectly motionless for minutes (players may mistake him for
  a pile of gym mats or coal); folds into spaces too small (lockers, dumbwaiter, under the stage, wall
  seams); and, once he notices you, moves in **sudden lurches of horrible speed**.
- **Speech:** only courtly, slow, formal lines. Roll call. "Thank you, Theresa." "Attendance is mandatory."
  "None shall be left behind." "Who is out of their seat?" He calls everyone by **full legal name**. Under
  his text: a low wet synthesized murmur, never words.
- **Smell (in text only):** pennies, wet wool, Christmas ham.

### Miss Ruth Adair — the Reflection
- 1979 music teacher, 34, brown hair pinned up, cardigan, cat-eye glasses. Appears **only in mirrors, glass
  and the compact**. Never physically present. Frantic, kind, exhausted. She communicates by writing on
  fogged glass and pointing. She tried to finish it in 1979 and couldn't. Her husk kneels in the vault, a
  red pen still in her hand.

### Kevin Hoyt — the Safety Patrol
- 12-year-old from 1979, small, in a blue windbreaker with a **fluorescent orange Safety Patrol belt** across
  the chest and a silver whistle. Now a pale, faceless-at-distance husk-boy that stands motionless at the end
  of halls. Turns his head to follow you. Never walks while observed.

### Denise Webb — the lure
- 15, 1979 marching band uniform (green & gold, shako hat in her lap), clarinet. In the gym she is an animated
  husk the school uses as bait: warm-looking at first, then wrong (she calls Marcus "Marcus Webb").
  Her real voice (the diary page) is brave and plain.

### Minor/off-screen
- **Walt Pruszynski** (log, notes), **Gerald Haupt** (letter), **Mr. Lutz** (biology teacher; cicada board),
  **Mrs. Ostrowski** (librarian; kept the case key in the card catalog, drawer "HA–HE"), **Maureen Dolan**
  (Tess's mom; signature on the form), **Grandma Ivy Webb** (photo inscription).

## 4. The rules of the school (diegetic; the whole design hangs on these)

Introduced through friends, notes and experience — never as a tutorial popup wall.

| # | Rule (as written in-world) | Mechanic | Taught by |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | "No student may be in the halls when the bell rings." | During a **period** the Colonel patrols halls and peeks into rooms. | Marcus (Ch2), Walt's log, Denise's diary quote |
| R2 | "Students will be seated and quiet during class." | **Seated + still = safe**, even if he looks right at you. Moving while he's near and watching = caught. | Ch2 (Leah lifts her head to film → taken) |
| R3 | "No running in the halls." | Running is loud (he investigates); **Safety Patrol** whistles if you run in its sight. | Ch3 basement (sleeping Colonel stirs), Ch5 long hall |
| R4 | "Hall passes are required." | The 2F long hall loops forever until you carry the **hall pass**. | Ch5 |
| R5 | "Attendance is mandatory." | Three roll calls ask "Theresa Dolan?" Answering marks you present (see endings). | Adair's mirror "DON'T ANSWER HIM", Walt's log |
| R6 | "No student may leave before dismissal." | Front doors loop back inside until the end. | Ch1 |
| R7 | (unwritten) "Only the school uses your full name." | Voices that use full legal names are **the school**, not your friends. | Prologue, Ch1 (Leah: "He never calls you Theresa.") |
| R8 | (Adair's) "Mirrors remember the last day." | The **compact** shows the room as it was on Oct 19, 1979. | Ch5 restroom mirror |

**Periods.** Signaled by the intercom: crackle → two-tone chime → (sometimes) the voice → ~10 s later the
**bell**. Hall fluorescents stutter on, green-white and buzzing. The period lasts ~60–90 s; ends with a
second bell, lights die, the Colonel withdraws into a wall seam. Hallway clocks run during periods; their
hands show progress toward the dismissal bell. Between periods = passing time (free exploration).

## 6. Story structure (chapters, beats, maps, gates)

Chapter title cards: `PROLOGUE — Kettle Run`, `I — After Hours`, `II — First Period`, `III — Custodial`,
`IV — Attendance`, `V — Upstairs`, `VI — Passing Time`, `VII — Assembly`, `VIII — The Register`, `EPILOGUE`.

Followers: friends trail Tess RPG-Maker style (caterpillar) when "in party." Friends comment on things Tess
examines (group banter) while in party.

### PROLOGUE — *Kettle Run* (≈6 min) — maps: `exterior`
1. Black. Text card: "Kettle Run, Pennsylvania. October 18, 1996." Rain; engine idling; wipers.
2. **Car scene** (CG `car_interior`, VN dialogue, ~30 lines, 2–3 flavor choices for Tess). Establish:
   Danny's legend (the Nine, the Colonel's gold, "Hollow Hell"), Leah's camcorder intro (full names; "Never
   call me Theresa on tape"), Marcus quiet, looking at the school, Danny's bad joke, Tess's private
   thought about the folded transfer form in her bag. Leah hands Tess the tape: "For the drive." Tess
   thinks she means tonight.
3. **Exterior** (player control, party of 4). Rain, steam venting from cracks (mine fire), a yellow
   `DANGER — UNDERGROUND MINE FIRE` sign, the dead oak covered in cicada shells (Leah: "Brood Two. They came
   up in May. Seventeen years underground."), the flagpole rope clanking, the sign `HALLOWELL CONSOLIDATED
   SCHOOL — HOME OF THE COLLIERS` with letters missing, **NEMO RELINQUATUR** over the doors (Leah translates:
   "Let no one be left behind." Danny: "Aw. That's sweet. Creepy, but sweet.").
4. Front doors chained → Danny's bolt cutters (scripted). They go in. Checkpoint.

### I — *After Hours* (≈8 min) — maps: `lobby`, `west1`
1. **Vestibule:** trophy case (glass reflects Tess; for a single frame someone stands behind her — easy to miss).
2. **Lobby:** the Colonel's huge gilt portrait over the auditorium doors (**seated** in a carved chair).
   Brass **dedication plaque** (FOUNDER'S DAY = Oct 19; Breaker 1894; dedicated 1923 — needed for the safe
   later). **Memorial board** "THE HOMECOMING NINE — FOREVER IN OUR HEARTS" with nine faded photos; Marcus
   lingers at Denise's. Auditorium doors: heavy chain + padlock. East-hall double doors: **jammed** (from
   the other side). Danny drops his **backpack** by the bench ("This thing weighs a ton").
3. **West hall 1F**, party exploring: Room 101 (kindergarten: tiny chairs, cubbies with names, crayon
   drawings "When I Grow Up"; Danny wedges into a tiny chair), Room 102 (desks scattered), Room 103, the
   main office (locked; through the reception window a single **red light** glows on the PA console),
   girls' restroom (mirror too grimy), library doors at the west end (stuck from inside), the west stairwell
   (collapsed — rubble; a landmark), a **bell schedule** on the bulletin board (doc).
4. **The disappearance** (trigger: after Tess has entered ≥2 rooms and walks past the `danny_vanish` anchor):
   Danny is mid-story ("—and my uncle Frank goes, Danny, there's things down there that—"). The player is
   prompted to examine the trophy/bulletin board beside them; Tess's flashlight flickers; Danny's line cuts
   off mid-word. Turn back: **Danny's red flashlight rolling slowly across the floor, still on, spinning to a
   stop.** Ambience drops to silence for 3 s. Leah: "Danny. Not funny." Tess picks up the flashlight (item).
5. Calling for him. From Room 102, Danny's voice: "Guys? ...Theresa? In here." Leah: "*Theresa?* He never
   calls you that." Room 102: **desks now in perfect rows**; chalkboard in fresh chalk: `DANIEL KOVACH —
   PRESENT`. Marcus: "Danny can't spell 'present.'"
6. They decide to get out and call the cops. Lobby → vestibule → front doors → **they step out of the library
   doors at the far west end of the west hall** (seamless transfer, no fade). Leah films it: "We walked in a
   straight line." Marcus: "Yeah."
7. Intercom crackle. Chapter ends.

### II — *First Period* (≈8 min) — maps: `west1`
1. Two-tone chime. The voice (the Colonel, styled text): "Good evening, students. You will proceed to your
   homerooms. Attendance will now be taken." Then the **bell**. Fluorescents stutter on down the hall.
2. Marcus (urgent, first time he's ever raised his voice): "Get in a room. Sit down. Now." Room 103 is closest.
   Player must **sit at a desk** (prompt: "Sit"). Leah sits too — but keeps the camcorder up.
3. **First glimpse (fragments):** through the narrow window in 103's door, light is blotted out by something
   passing; brass glints; the drag; the breathing. The door opens. Fixed camera; player input disabled except
   that the screen tells you nothing — silence. Only the bottom edge of a vast navy coat, the fringe of a
   crimson sash, split boots under fluorescent flicker. **CG `colonel_hand`:** an enormous ringed hand rests
   on Tess's desk beside her face. Tess portrait: eyes squeezed shut. A long breath. The click of Leah's
   record button. The hand lifts. A soft wet sound; a chair scraping. Silence. The dismissal bell. Lights die.
4. Leah's desk is empty. **Her camcorder** sits on it, red LED still on. (Item.) **VHS playback** (overlay
   `vhs`): grainy frames, timestamp `OCT 18 1996 10:41PM`: Tess and Marcus heads-down; a dark mass fills the
   frame; a huge hand settles on Leah's shoulder; the image tilts; static; **one frame of the Colonel's small
   serene face very close to the lens, smiling** (≈0.4 s); blue screen `▶ PLAY`. (First sight of his face.)
5. Marcus tells Tess about Denise (choice-driven depth; he shows the photo). His grandmother read Denise's
   diary: *"When the bell rings, get in a room and sit down. Walt says so."* Objective: **get into the main
   office — the intercom.** Marcus: "Janitor would've had keys." The east-hall doors (lobby) now hang open.
   Checkpoint.

### III — *Custodial* (≈10 min) — maps: `lobby`, `east1`, `basement` (party: Marcus)
1. **East hall 1F:** cafeteria (long tables, trays, a 1979 banner `GO COLLIERS! HOMECOMING '79`), kitchen
   (walk-in freezer — warm inside; industrial mixer; the **dumbwaiter**: Danny's voice up the shaft,
   "Theresa... it's so dark down here..." Marcus: "That's not him."), boys' restroom, **gym doors blocked**
   (fallen bleachers inside), **east stairwell** — up is barred by a padlocked **accordion security gate**;
   down to the basement is open.
2. **Basement:** dripping, steam pipes hissing, puddles (footsteps splash), caged bulbs dead. The **boiler**
   is warm and lit — fire glow through the firebox door. Impossible. The **coal bin**: a great dark heap of coal
   and, in it, **something motionless and enormous half-buried, breathing very slowly** (examine: "Old gym
   mats? ...It's breathing."). Marcus whispers: "Walk. Don't run." Running within ~8 tiles makes it stir
   (coal slides, breathing quickens); a second disturbance → it rises → caught (game over). The **coal
   chute** in the bin's wall: rusted grate, padlocked (bolt cutters later → TRUANT ending).
3. **Janitor's office** (padlocked hasp; spare key on the ledge above the door — examine the doorframe;
   Marcus hints after 40 s): Walt's cot, radio, a calendar stuck on **OCTOBER 1979** with the 19th circled,
   **Walt's log** (doc), a pegboard of labeled hooks — most empty — and the **janitor's key ring** (keys:
   `OFFICE`, `2F GATE`). Storage room (optional; batteries, piles of old desks), electrical room
   (optional; breaker panel — flipping BSMT CORR lights two caged bulbs with a loud CLUNK that makes the coal
   bin stir once).
4. **First free period** begins as they climb back to the east hall (periods now active on 1F). The coal
   bin is empty afterward — only a body-shaped hollow in the coal. Checkpoint.

### IV — *Attendance* (≈8 min) — maps: `west1` (office suite), `lobby`
1. **Main office** (OFFICE key): reception counter, **sign-out sheet** for Oct 19, 1979 (Denise Webb —
   "dentist" — signed I. Webb — *time out* blank), teacher mailboxes (Adair's has her note to Haupt:
   "Gerald, cancel the assembly. I am begging you. —R."), lost & found (battery). **Nurse's office:** cot,
   eye chart, a jar labeled `BROOD II — 1979 — MR. LUTZ` full of cicada shells, the newspaper clipping.
   **Principal Haupt's office:** desk, a **small portrait of the Colonel** on the wall (the safe is hidden
   behind it — not discoverable yet), filing cabinet (disciplinary records: "In hall without pass — reported
   by K. Hoyt, Safety Patrol").
2. **PA room:** the intercom console (UI `pa`). LISTEN to rooms: `205` Danny muttering "...i will not leave...
   i will not leave..."; `LIB` soft crying, then "...T?" (Leah, real); `AUD` a crowd murmuring, old
   applause, a band tuning; `GYM` slow wet breathing; `101` small voices singing the alma mater; `BSMT` the
   boiler roaring; `OFFICE` your own breathing; others static.
3. The console switches itself to ALL CALL. **Roll call #1**: "Daniel Kovach." (faint) "...present." "Leah
   Brandt." (crying) "...present." "Marcus Webb." Marcus stares; says nothing. "Marcus Webb." Marcus to Tess:
   "Don't." "**Theresa Dolan?**" → choice **[Here.] / [(Say nothing.)]**. Here → "Thank you, Theresa." Silence →

## 13. Art direction (for all art agents)

- **Resolution:** world 480×270, 16 px tiles, characters 24×32 frames, the Colonel ~56×64 frames.
  Portraits 96×120 px (displayed at 2× in the 960×540 UI layer). CGs 480×270.
- **Pixel discipline:** crisp, no anti-aliased blur, no gradients that read as "vector." Dithering is allowed and
  encouraged for grime and light falloff. Limited palette (TECH.md `palette.js`). Consistent light direction
  (top-left), 1-px dark outlines on characters (selective outlining allowed).
- **The school is tactile:** peeling two-tone paint (institutional green below a chair rail, cream above), cracked
  linoleum checker, water stains on ceiling-tile edges, rust on lockers, scratched desks with carved initials,
  curling posters, dead fluorescent tubes, dust, leaf litter blown in, graffiti from earlier trespassers
  ("HOLLOW HELL", "KR '91", a pentagram drawn badly). Each tile key needs 2–4 variants and a **clean 1979
  variant** (for the compact view).
- **Animation:** blinking/dying lights, flicker, swaying cables, dripping water + ripples, steam, rain streaks on
  windows, dust motes, idle animations (Tess shifts weight, pushes hair back; Danny bounces; Leah adjusts glasses;
  Marcus hums — small head bob), the Colonel's slow breathing (body swell).
- **Portrait consistency:** one rig per character; expressions change brows/eyes/mouth/head tilt/gaze/sweat/
  tears/blush and lighting — never the design. Lighting variants: `normal`, `dark` (flashlight side-lit),
  `under` (lit from below), `fluor` (green-white), `red` (breaker), `dawn`. Grime level 0–2 across the night.
- **Horror visuals:** selective — chromatic split and wave distortion during supernatural moments, a vignette
  that tightens near the Colonel, a VHS filter for tape playback, the compact's round mirror frame with a crack.
  Provide a "reduce flashing" option.

## 14. Audio direction

- Everything synthesized with WebAudio (no samples). Mixer buses: master, music, sfx, ambience (+ UI).
- **Ambience per area:** exterior rain/wind/distant thunder/steam hiss; halls: near-silence with building creaks,
  distant drips, wind at windows; periods: fluorescent buzz (60/120 Hz + crackle); basement: boiler roar, pipe
  knocks, drips, low rumble; library: deep hush, clock tick; gym: rain drumming on the roof, echo; auditorium:
  vast room tone, faint crowd murmur during the Assembly; vault/breaker: low fire roar, wood creak, distant
  machinery, ember crackle.
- **Footsteps by surface:** linoleum, wood, tile, concrete, carpet, metal, water, coal, gravel, grass, asphalt,
  stone. Running = louder, faster.
- **Signature sounds:** two-tone intercom chime; the school bell (electro-mechanical, long, harsh); intercom crackle
  + hum; the Colonel's breath, drag, medal jingle, "hm?"; safety whistle; glass-harmonica tone (Adair); husk rustle;
  door creaks (wood, metal), locker clank, chain rattle, padlock, key turn, bolt cutters snap, camcorder click/
  whir, VHS tracking noise, typewriter-ish TRS-80 key clicks + beep, safe dial clicks, stamp thunk, page turns,
  flashlight click, battery swap, lightning crack + rolling thunder, wind gust, rain on windows.
- **Music (sparse):** title: detuned music-box alma mater; low drones per act (pitch-shifted further each act);
  phonograph alma mater (warped, crackle); chase: pulsing sub-drone + irregular metallic hits (no melody);
  ending: a quiet lo-fi guitar-like figure (Leah's tape) for good endings, nothing but room tone for PRESENT.
- **Spatial:** stereo pan + distance falloff + lowpass when occluded by walls. Some sounds should make the player
  *stop and listen* (the Colonel above you; a door opening two rooms away).
- **Silence is a sound:** scripted drop-outs before major beats (Danny's vanish, the locker, the Register).
