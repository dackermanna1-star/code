## 0. Ground rules

### 0.1 Confidence tags
- **[V]**: checked against a source (ru.wikipedia, mos.ru, GOST, period press).
- **[≈]**: plausible estimate. Use it as the game value and tune by eye or ear.
- **[AD]**: art-direction decision made when merging the research. It resolves a conflict or adapts reality to voxels.
- Untagged values come from period knowledge and are reliable enough to build with.

### 0.2 Voxel conventions
- 1 vox = 0.25 m, so 4 vox = 1 m. To convert, multiply metres by 4 and round to the nearest whole voxel.
- **Floor-to-floor height is 11 vox (2.75 m) for every series [AD].** Real values are 2.75–2.85 m: a 2.64 m ceiling plus a 0.14 m slab. 10 vox is too low even for khrushchyovkas.
- Exterior walls are 1–2 vox (real 0.30–0.40 m). The window recess is 1 vox (real 0.15–0.25 m). Build buildings as hollow shells 1–2 vox thick.
- **Stairs [AD]:** the real stair is a 0.155 m riser on a 0.30 m tread (≈27°). The voxel stair is a 1-vox riser (0.25 m) on a 2-vox tread (0.50 m), which gives 26.6°. That means 11 risers per floor, split into flights of 5 and 6. The player can step up 1 vox.
- Anything thinner than a voxel is a decal or texture: panel seams (20–30 mm), the 0.15 m kerb reveal (treat it as a 1-vox step), plates and stickers.

### 0.3 Era checklist (wrong for 2008, never use)
- Police are **«МИЛИЦИЯ»** (militsiya). The force was renamed «полиция» only in 2011.
- Housing is managed by the **ДЕЗ / ГУ ИС района …** (the district housing directorate). The «Жилищник» organisations do not exist yet.
- **No metro tokens.** Moscow withdrew them in Jan–Feb 1999 [V]. Tickets are paper magnetic-stripe or contactless «Ультралайт» (Ultralight).
- No 10-rub coins; they arrive late in 2009. Coins are 1, 2 and 5 rub plus kopecks.
- None of the following, which all came later:
  - the unified 2013–14 city navigation;
  - the Lebedev metro "М" (2014);
  - tactile platform strips;
  - «Жилищник» uniforms.
- No outdoor tobacco advertising (banned in 2007).
- Beer ads carry a health warning covering at least 10% of the area.
- Slot-machine halls exist until July 2009.
- Russia still changes clocks for daylight saving until 2011. Winter time is UTC+3.
- No "sovietwave" synth music. Kiosks and taxis play radio pop and chanson.
- Emergency numbers are 01 fire, 02 militsiya, 03 ambulance, 04 gas.
- Phone number formats: 8-495-xxx-xx-xx (landline), 8-916/903/926-xxx-xx-xx (mobile), +7916… inside SMS.

### 1.2 Sky, time and weather

**Sun times:**
- 22 December: sunrise 08:59, sunset 15:57–15:58.
- Twilight ends: civil 16:55, nautical 17:50, astronomical 18:50.
- Blue hour runs ≈16:10–17:10. The sun sinks only ≈6°/h at this latitude.
- Mid-January sunset is ≈16:20.
- December averages ≈18 h of sunshine for the whole month, so **overcast is the default**.

| Sky state | Zenith | Mid | Horizon | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blue hour, clear | `#1C2B4F` | `#2E4775` | `#5A6B95` | SW band `#8E7A8C` → `#C49A86` |
| Blue hour, overcast (most common) | `#3B4560` | n/a | `#66708A` | |

| Clear frost | `#0E1424` | n/a | `#3A3040` | |

The overcast night sky is a dirty pink-orange-mauve dome. It is brightest toward the centre and toward big junctions, darkest at the zenith, and shows no stars.

**Weather modes:**
- **Snowglow.** Snow throws lamp light up, the low cloud sends it back down, and the two bounce repeatedly. Measured near Berlin at more than 180× amplification, brighter than twice a full moon (Jechow & Hölker 2019).
  - Ambient multiplier: 1.0 on a clear night without snow, 3–5 on an overcast night with fresh snow.
  - Shadows become soft and almost directionless.
- **Clear frost (−15 to −25 °C).** Darker, with a black-blue zenith.
  - **Light pillars (световые столбы):** vertical beams 10–30° tall above lamps, in each lamp's colour.
  - Heat-plant plumes rise straight up, lit orange-grey from below.
  - "Diamond dust" crystals glint inside lamp cones.
- **Thaw (−2 to +2 °C).** Fog, wet snow, slush, black wet asphalt with long orange reflections.
  - Visibility 100–300 m, and lamp cones look solid.

**Falling snow:**
- Flakes are visible almost only **inside lamp cones** and against lit windows. Against a bright sky they read as **dark specks**. Backlit flakes glow strongly because snow scatters light forward.
- Fall speeds: dendrites 0.5–1.0 m/s, aggregates 1–1.5 m/s, wet snow 1.5–2 m/s, graupel 1–3 m/s.
- Density: 100–300 flakes/m³ in moderate snow.

**Visibility in snow** (Koschmieder relation, V = 3.912/σ):

| Snowfall | Visibility | Extinction σ |
|---|---|---|
| Light | ≈1 km | 0.004 /m |
| Moderate | ≈400 m | 0.01 /m |
| Heavy | ≈150 m | 0.026 /m |
| Blizzard | ≈50 m | 0.08 /m |

**Game default: σ ≈ 0.012–0.02.**

- **Plinth:** 0.8–1.4 m high (3–6 vox), dark or tiled. Square vents (продухи) 0.25 × 0.25 m (1 vox) every few metres. A cat often sits in one, sometimes with a note: «Не закрывайте продухи — там живут кошки» ("Don't block the vents, cats live here"). The vents steam in winter.
- **Seams:** horizontal at every floor line, vertical at every bay (2.7–3.6 m). Patchily re-sealed seams show as darker vertical streaks.

- Sill at 0.8 m (3 vox). One window per room, repeating every 3.0–3.6 m (12–14 vox).
- **Frames in 2008 [AD]:** 50–60% are old double wooden frames painted white (flaking, grey `#D8D4C8`). The rest are white PVC, plus a few brown "wood-look" PVC `#5A3A26`.
  - Old frames have a ≈0.13 m gap stuffed with cotton wool and sealed with paper tape for winter, and a форточка (top vent pane) ≈0.4 × 0.5 m that stays open even at −15 °C.
- **Frost:** fern patterns (морозные узоры) grow from the bottom corners of the outer pane of old frames. Single-glazed stairwell windows, often with one pane replaced by plywood, frost heavily. PVC stays clear but fogs along the bottom edge.
- **Window grilles** on floor 1, and often floor 2: welded steel "sunburst" (солнышко) pattern, painted white or black.

### 2.5 Balconies and loggias

- **Balcony:** slab projects 0.8–1.2 m (3–5 vox) and is 2.4–3.6 m wide (10–14 vox).
  - Railing or parapet 1.0–1.1 m high (4 vox).
  - Fronts are concrete panels (often ribbed) or steel bars `#3A3A36`.
- **Loggia:** recessed 1.2–1.5 m (5–6 vox).
- **Glazing:** 70–80% are glazed, each one differently. Randomise per unit:
  - aluminium sliders, white `#F2F2EE` or bare silver;
  - old white wooden frames, sometimes missing a pane and patched with plywood or cardboard;
  - new white PVC;
  - sheet cladding below the glass: beige siding `#D9CBA8`, white profiled sheet, blue corrugated `#4E6E9A`, or wooden «вагонка» lining;
  - extensions (выносы) pushed 20–30 cm (1 vox) past the slab edge;
  - pitched corrugated mini-roofs on the top floor, with icicles.
- **Unglazed balconies:** laundry frozen stiff, skis, a sled, three-litre jars, a tyre, an old armchair, a bicycle under snow.
- **Other facade clutter:**
  - «ТВ-Плюс» satellite dishes 0.55–0.6 m: 1–3 per section, on 5–10% of balconies.
  - Split air-conditioner units: 1–5 per facade (rare in the outskirts).

### 2.6 Roofs

- Flat bitumen roof with internal drains [V]. Parapet 0.6–1.2 m (2–5 vox) with galvanised flashing `#9AA0A3`.
- **Lift machine room:** a brick or concrete box over each entrance, 2.5–3 m above the roof (10–12 vox), 3 × 5 m in plan (12 × 20 vox), with louvres and a steel door. On 9-storey blocks it sits on top of the technical attic.
- **Roof hatch:** padlocked, sign «Выход на кровлю закрыт. Ключи в ДЕЗ» ("Roof exit locked. Keys at the DEZ").
- **Antennas:** collective Yagi ("wave channel") rods on 3–4 m masts (12–16 vox) with guy wires, plus rusty abandoned dipoles. Cable-TV and ISP cables draped over the parapet.
- **Winter:**
  - Snow cornices 0.3–0.5 m (1–2 vox) overhang the parapet; icicles up to 1 m (4 vox).
  - Signs: «Осторожно! Сход снега и наледи» ("Caution: falling snow and ice") or «Осторожно! Сход снега и падение сосулек» ("Caution! Falling snow and icicles").
  - Red-and-white tape on posts below the facade.

### 2.8 House-number plates

- **Dominant (pre-2013 Moscow tradition) [AD]:** white letters on blue `#1F4E96` [mos.ru]. Usually a corner plate or illuminated box ≈0.9 × 0.5 m (4 × 2 vox), often dead or cracked.
  - Street name on top in a condensed sans: «ул. Академика Ветрова» ("Academician Vetrov St.").
  - Big number below: «14», with «корп. 2» or «к. 2» ("block 2") smaller.
- **Variants:** illuminated boxes with a white face and black letters, often unlit. Old white enamel plates with black letters survive on khrushchyovkas.
- Never use the unified post-2014 design.
- **Address formats:** «д. 14, корп. 2» ("building 14, block 2"), «стр. 1» ("structure 1", non-residential), «вл. 5» ("land holding 5"). Microdistrict example: «Тополиный б-р, д. 7, корп. 3» ("Poplar Blvd, bldg 7, block 3").
- **Painted end-wall numbers:** 1–2 m tall (4–8 vox) near the top of a blank end wall, e.g. «14/2» or «КОРП. 2» ("BLOCK 2").

### 3.1 Surfaces

- **Ceiling:** black soot spots from burnt matches that kids flicked up to stick.
- **Floor:** metlakh tile 150 × 150 or 100 × 100 mm. Either a brown `#7A4A32` and beige `#C9B28E` checkerboard or plain red-brown, with chipped and missing tiles.
- **Flights:** precast terrazzo-concrete `#8C8A84` with flecks.
- **Stair geometry:**
  - Real: 2 flights of 9 risers per floor (≈0.155 × 0.30 m); in voxels see §0.2.
  - Flight width 1.05–1.2 m (4–5 vox).
  - Dark steel balusters; brown PVC handrail `#5A3A28`, often missing or melted.
- **Half-landing window:** single-glazed, painted many times. Some panes cracked or replaced with plywood. On the sill: a coffee or sprat tin used as an ashtray and a dead plant.

### 3.2 Landing layouts

- **9-storey (II-49, 1-515):** 4 flat doors per landing. The lift is in the stair core and the chute on the half-landing.
- **P-44, P-3, KOPE:**
  1. A lift lobby with 2–3 lifts and the chute.
  2. A corridor to 4 flats that residents have closed off with their own steel lobby door (тамбурная дверь).
  3. An exit onto an **open-air transfer balcony** leading to the **smoke-proof stair (незадымляемая лестница)**. Transfer balconies are required on buildings over 28 m, so on every floor you step out into wind and snow.
- **Basement and attic doors:** padlocked, sign «Посторонним вход воспрещён» ("No unauthorised entry").

### 5.4 Trolleybus wires

- One pair per direction (+ and −), 0.6 m apart, at **5.8 m** nominal height (range 5.5–6.3 m; 23 vox), over the right-hand lane 3–4 m from the kerb.
- **Span cables (поперечины):** every 25–35 m, attached at 7–7.5 m (28–30 vox) to 9–10 m poles (36–40 vox) standing 0.5–0.8 m from the kerb.
- Curves are polygonal, held by fans of pull-off cables (оттяжки).
- Switches ("frogs", стрелки) have a small box on the span. Crossing pieces (пересечения) where lines cross. Section insulators about every 1 km.
- **Wire:** grooved copper ≈12 mm, rendered as a thin line `#3A3530`.
- **Vehicle trolley poles:** ≈6 m long, mounted on the roof at ≈3.4 m, raised 20–25°. Rope catchers (уловители) at the back of the roof.
- **Winter:** arcing at frogs and in freezing drizzle (§1.1 colours), with a crackle and a metallic "zing" running along the wire as a trolleybus approaches.

### 7.1 Corrections to the brief
- **No tokens.** They were withdrawn in Jan–Feb 1999 [V]; St Petersburg kept them longer. In 2008 the change underway was from magnetic-stripe paper tickets to contactless **«Ультралайт»** paper tickets (MIFARE Ultralight). Contactless tickets were introduced on 1 Feb 2007 and replaced the 1-, 2- and 5-trip magnetic tickets in early 2008 [V].
- A coin or token slot on a turnstile is an anachronism. A dusty token in a drawer or under a radiator works as an Easter egg.
- Escalators are uncommon at 1970s–80s shallow stations; most have **stairs only**.

### 7.5 Interval clock (интервальные часы) [V]

- Mounted above the tunnel mouth at the departure end of the platform. It is meant for drivers, but everyone watches it.
- **The left half shows the time; the right half shows time since the previous train left.** The interval resets when the departing train enters the tunnel.
- Introduced 7 Sep 1947; dials were later replaced by digital displays. All clocks are synchronised from the Central Electric Clock Station (ЦЭЧС).
- **Build [≈]:** black housing `#0B0B0B`, 1.6 × 0.35 m (6–7 × 1–2 vox). Red 7-segment digits `#FF3A1E`, 0.15–0.2 m tall, with a faint glow.
- Example reading: `23:41 | 6:12`. At night the right side climbs slowly, which suits an empty platform.

### 7.6 Entrances

- Outskirts stations usually have **no surface building**. You reach them down stairs into an underpass (§5.9), with kiosks, leading to an underground vestibule.
- **Pavilion variant:** a low concrete-and-glass box with aluminium-framed glazing and «МЕТРО» in large red letters along the roof edge.
- **The big red М:** a sans-serif "М" with slightly flared stems, 1.0–1.5 m tall (4–6 vox), on a 3–4 m pylon (12–16 vox) or on the pavilion, lit from inside. Often paired with a light box carrying the station name. Variants are mixed in 2008.
- **Doors:** heavy glass **pendulum doors** in wood or aluminium frames with brass push plates. People hold them for the person behind; they close with a deep "whump".
- **Winter details:** a warm-air curtain hisses at the doors; steam forms where warm air meets −10 °C; grey rubber mats or flattened cardboard lie on wet granite; grey slush is tracked in.
- **Ventilation kiosks** stand over the line in yards and parks: louvred concrete boxes ≈3 × 3 × 3 m (12³ vox) breathing warm damp air and steam, with a low fan drone. A strong atmospheric prop.

## 8. Soundscape synthesis recipes

All values are [≈] unless tagged; tune by ear.

### 8.0 Global mix

- **Snow acoustics:**
  - Fresh powder absorbs strongly (α 0.6–0.9 above 500 Hz). Courtyards sound "cotton-wool" quiet, RT60 0.6–1.0 s.
  - Packed snow, ice and slush reflect, so RT60 rises to 1.5–2.5 s.
  - While fresh snow lies, apply a global high-shelf of −6 dB above 3 kHz.
- **Courtyard flutter echo between parallel slabs:**
  - Delay = 2·d/343: ≈0.23–0.41 s for 40–70 m spacing.
  - 2–4 feedback taps at −8 dB per tap, lowpassed at 2.5 kHz.
- **Distance:**
  - −6 dB per doubling.
  - Lowpass fc ≈ 16000/(1 + d/40) Hz, with d in metres. Beyond 200 m almost nothing above 2 kHz survives.
  - Frosty inversion nights carry trains, the MKAD ring road and dogs 1–2 km.
- **Ambient bed:** courtyard at night 38–45 dBA; next to a prospekt 60–68 dBA.

### 8.1 Wind and snow

Average winter wind is 3–4 m/s, with gusts of 10–15 m/s in the gaps between slabs.

- **Bed:** brown noise → lowpass 250–500 Hz (Q 0.7), gain following a gust value U.
- **Gust LFO:** random target every 6–20 s, 2–5 s glides, plus a 0.3–1 Hz sub-gust at ±3 dB.
- **Aeolian whistles** (f ≈ 0.2·U/d):
  - 5 mm wire ≈320 Hz at 8 m/s; 7 mm antenna coax ≈230 Hz; 16 mm balcony rail ≈100 Hz.
  - Recipe: white noise → bandpass Q 40–80, centre f = k·U so the pitch rises in gusts. Gate in only above 6 m/s.
- **Corner howl:** bandpasses at 180 / 410 / 620 Hz, Q 15, −12 dB.
- **Blowing snow:** highpass 4 kHz noise at −36 dB, amplitude ∝ U³.
- **Rattles** of roof tin or balcony sheet: random 15 ms impacts of bandpassed noise 300–1500 Hz whenever U > 0.7.
- **Plastic bag in a tree:** 8–14 Hz AM noise at 1–3 kHz.
- **Snow falling off a branch («плюх», "plop"):**
  1. Noise burst → lowpass 600 Hz, 20 ms attack, 200 ms decay.
  2. Powder trail: bandpass 2–6 kHz, 0.6–1.5 s.
  3. Branch spring-back: 8–12 ticks of 1 ms.

### 8.2 Birds and dogs

- **Hooded crow (серая ворона)**, the signature winter bird [V: "hoarse caw"]. Flocks of hundreds stream to roost at 15:50–16:40.
  - Caw «каарр»: 0.3–0.6 s, sawtooth f0 ≈550 Hz gliding 600 → 480 Hz, ±30 Hz random FM at 80–150 Hz, tanh shaper, formant bandpasses at 1.2 and 2.3 kHz (Q 4).
  - Envelope: 25 ms attack, sustain, 80 ms release.
  - Phrasing: 2–6 caws 0.5–0.9 s apart, answered from other roofs.
  - Take-off: 4–6 lowpass-400 Hz thumps at 5 Hz.
- **Jackdaw (галка)** «кья»: f0 1.0–1.4 kHz, 0.12 s, in flocks.
- **Magpie:** noise bursts at 12 Hz for 0.6 s.
- **Daytime only:** great tit «зинь-зинь» at 5–7 kHz; sparrows.
- **Dogs** (strays at garage co-ops, chained dogs at building sites):
  - Bark 80–180 ms: sawtooth 500 → 350 Hz (big dogs 250 → 180 Hz) plus a 10 ms noise onset; formants at 600 and 1300 Hz.
  - Phrasing: runs of 3–8 barks at 2–3 Hz, then 5–40 s of silence; answered from another direction.
  - Howl: 1.5–3 s, sine 450 → 600 → 420 Hz with 5 Hz vibrato.
  - Beyond 150 m: lowpass 1.2 kHz plus the courtyard echo.

### 8.3 Footsteps

- **Walk:** 1.8 Hz (556 ms period, ±15 ms jitter), alternating L/R by ±1.5 dB. Matches a decal stride of 0.72 m.
- **Stairs:** 2.2 Hz.
- **Each step:** heel strike, then toe-off 60–120 ms later at −6 dB. Use one precomputed white-noise buffer with a random offset per grain, through a BiquadFilter and Gain envelope.
- **Door stamp:** 2–3 heavy stamps at 2.5 Hz, ≈80 Hz thumps.

| Surface | Recipe | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh snow, −2 to −8 °C | 30–60 grains (1–3 ms bandpassed noise, 1.2–2.5 kHz), density rising then falling; 90 Hz sine thump 40 ms | 220–300 ms |
| Fresh snow below −10 °C (squeak starts ≈−8 °C) | as above, plus the **скрип** ("squeak"): 2–4 resonant bandpass bursts at 1.5–3 kHz, Q 12, 30–60 ms | 250 ms |
| Packed snow / path | 15–25 grains at 2–3.5 kHz; squeak dominant, small thump | 120–160 ms |
| Ice | 5 ms noise → bandpass 3 kHz, plus a 2 kHz ring decaying in 30 ms. Slip: 300–600 ms scrape bandpassed 1–3 kHz. Puddle crack: broadband impulse plus 3–4 ringing filters at 3–6 kHz, 60 ms. | 40–80 ms |
| Salted slush (каша) | lowpass 1.5 kHz noise plus 3–6 squelch chirps falling 900 → 300 Hz, 20 ms each; toe-off suction 60 ms bandpassed 400 Hz swell | 280–350 ms |
| Stairwell concrete | rubber-sole thump 120 Hz 35 ms, plus sand-and-salt grit 40 ms highpass 3 kHz; RT60 2–3 s, bright | 60 ms + reverb |

### 8.4 Traffic and cars

- **Distant drone:**
  - Brown noise → lowpass 300 Hz, plus pink noise → bandpass 900 Hz (Q 0.6), slow AM at 0.05–0.2 Hz.
  - Wet roads: add highpass 2 kHz spray «шшш» ("shhh") at +6 dB relative to dry.
- **Pass-by at 50–100 m:** Gaussian envelope over 3–6 s, tyre peak at 800–1200 Hz.
- **Engines:**
  - 4-cylinder firing frequency = rpm/30: Lada at 800 rpm ≈27 Hz, at 2500 rpm ≈83 Hz.
- **Studded tyres** (most cars in 2008): granular crackle at 3–6 kHz; slow on ice it becomes discrete clicks at ≈15·v Hz (v in km/h).
- **Ice crunch** in courtyards at 5–10 km/h: grains lowpassed at 500 Hz, 100–200 grains/s, plus ice pops (impulse → bandpass 2–4 kHz) at 2–8/s and snow squeak at 1.8 kHz.
- **Stuck Lada:** wheelspin (sine 300 → 900 Hz plus noise) and engine revving.
- **Car alarm** («Пантеон», «Шерифф-М»):
  - ≈120 dB at 1 m. Square waves → bandpass 1–3 kHz → soft clip. Set off by passing trucks or falling snow.
  - **Six-tone cycle, ≈4 s each, 25–30 s total, then silence:**
    1. Slow wail 700 → 1700 → 700 Hz, period 1.8 s.
    2. Fast yelp: saw-up 700 → 1700 Hz every 0.25 s.
    3. Hi-lo: alternating 1000 and 1400 Hz, 0.35 s each.
    4. "Machine gun": 1.6 kHz pulses at 12 Hz.
    5. Whoop: glide 400 → 1800 Hz over 0.6 s, repeated.
    6. Ray gun: glide 2500 → 600 Hz over 0.1 s, at 6 Hz.
  - Arm: 1 chirp. Disarm: 2 chirps. Chirps are 1.5–2.5 kHz, 80 ms. Indicator relay «тик-так» at 1.5 Hz.

### 8.5 Trolleybus (ZiU-682, DC motor, rheostat-contactor control РКСУ)

**Pull-away, in order:**
1. Line contactor «КЛАЦ» ("clack"): impulse plus modes at 180 / 650 / 1800 Hz, 150 ms.
2. Rheostat controller ratchet: 8–15 clicks at 4–8/s.
3. **Whine:** gear mesh ≈11 Hz per km/h (≈660 Hz at 60 km/h; perceived range ≈200–1200 Hz).
   - Saw at −20 dB, 2nd harmonic at −26 dB, ×4.3 partial at −32 dB.
   - Bandpass 300 Hz–3 kHz.

**Other sounds:**
- **Coasting:** the whine fades; contactors drop with «клац-клац».
- **Compressor at stops [AD]:** rattling chug «тр-тр-тр»: noise AM at 14–18 Hz (older units a slower 8–10 Hz thump), lowpass 800 Hz, 15–30 s on and off.
- **Doors:** «пшш» hiss (highpass 2 kHz noise, 0.6 s decay), then folding-door clatter.
- **Overhead:**
  - Continuous shoe hiss on the wire (bandpass 3 kHz, low level).
  - Double «тынц-тынц» at switches and crossings.
  - In frost: arcs of 20–200 ms made of 10–40 random broadband pops, synced to the visual flash.
- **Autoinformer:** «Осторожно, двери закрываются. Следующая остановка — „Улица Академика Ветрова“» ("Caution, doors closing. Next stop: Academician Vetrov Street").

### 8.6 Buses, marshrutkas, trains

- **LiAZ V8 diesel idle [AD]:** 600–650 rpm, so firing ≈40–43 Hz (this replaces the uncomputed 22–28 Hz figure). Ikarus 6-cylinder ≈30–33 Hz.
  - Recipe: pulse train at the firing frequency with ±2% jitter → lowpass 400 Hz, plus resonant peaks at 120 and 250 Hz. "Clatter": 3 ms noise bursts bandpassed 1–3 kHz, one per firing, at −14 dB. Plus body rattle.
  - Pull-away: rpm ramps to 1800–2000 over 5 s (≈130 Hz firing); an automatic gear shift dips 300 rpm.
  - Brake release: «пшшш», 0.4 s.
- **Marshrutka:** petrol 4-cylinder idle ≈27 Hz. The signature is the **sliding side door**: 0.5 s roller rumble (lowpass 400 Hz with rattle), then a heavy slam with modes at 90 / 210 / 480 Hz.
- **Metro heard through the ground or a building:** brown noise → lowpass 70 Hz plus sines at 31 and 47 Hz. Swell with 6–8 s attack, 4 s hold, 8–10 s release. Faint «та-дам» bogie clicks at 1–2 Hz on jointed track.
- **81-717 at the platform:**
  - **Departure:**
    1. Short air hiss and a hard double-leaf slam (80–150 Hz transient plus metallic rattle). There are **no door-warning beeps**.
    2. 12–18 contactor clicks over the first ≈10 s.
    3. Traction **whine** at ≈9 Hz per km/h (≈540 Hz at 60, ≈725 Hz at 80; gear ratio ≈5.33, 15-tooth pinion, both [≈]). 2nd harmonic at −12 dB, with slight volume steps at each controller notch. The stepped, slightly "sour" whine is what makes the 81-717 recognisable.
  - **Braking:**
    1. Whine falls from 600 to 100 Hz over 8–10 s.
    2. Narrowband brake squeal at 2–4 kHz below ≈8 km/h.
    3. Final «пшш»: noise highpassed at 2 kHz, 0.7 s.
  - **Compressor** (standing at the platform): «тук-тук-тук» pulses at 12–16 Hz over a 50–100 Hz drone, 20–40 s, ending in a hiss.
  - **Wheels:** a double «та-дах» per bogie at insulated rail joints.
  - **Arrival from the tunnel:**
    - Brown noise building over ≈10 s through a lowpass opening from 300 to 1500 Hz.
    - A piston-wind gust moves litter and hair; headlights show in the tunnel before the sound peaks.
  - **In-car noise:** 85–95 dB in reality; mix it as the dominant rumble.
  - **Horn:** a short, harsh ≈350 Hz blast when someone is near the edge.
- **Station details:**
  - Pendulum door «whump» ≈80 Hz.
  - АКП-73 flap «БАМ!» ("BAM!"): metal transient plus a pneumatic thump.
  - Escalator comb clack ≈1.9 Hz (0.75 m/s over 0.4 m treads), motor hum at 50 and 100 Hz, occasional handrail squeak.
  - Vent-kiosk low fan drone.
- **PA chain:** bandpass 300–3500 Hz, light saturation, RT60 2–3 s on marble and granite.
- **Optional PA chime «динь-дон»**, before station PA only. No fixed 2008 chime is verified, and none plays before «Осторожно, двери закрываются».
  - E5 659.3 Hz then C5 523.3 Hz, each 0.7 s, decay τ ≈0.35 s.
  - Partials 1.0, 2.0 (0.3), 2.76 (0.12, bell-like inharmonic).
- **Elektrichka (distant suburban train):** «тифон» horn of two tones ≈350/440 Hz, 1–2 s, then fading «та-дам … та-дам».

### 8.7 Snow clearing

- **КДМ diesel:** idle 25–35 Hz fundamental plus harmonics, ≈60 Hz under load.
- **Blade on asphalt:** bandpassed noise 800–3000 Hz with random grit pulses (tractor blade: broadband 200–3000 Hz with gritty AM at 30 Hz).
- **Hopper spinner:** 6–10 Hz rattle.
- **Reverse beeper:** ≈1 kHz, 0.5 s on, 0.5 s off.
- **MTZ tractor:** 4-cylinder diesel ≈30 Hz idle.
- **КО-206 snow loader:** «лапы» ("paws") clank at ≈1 Hz over conveyor rumble, with dump trucks queued alongside.
- **Janitor tools:**
  - Steel scraper or pusher: 800–4000 Hz scrape with 20–60 Hz gritty AM, 1.5–3 s per push, cycle 4–6 s.
  - Plywood or plastic shovel: 300–1500 Hz, ending in a hollow knock when tapped clean.
  - Ice chopper «тюк» every 0.9–1.2 s: click at 1–5 kHz plus 100 Hz thud plus tinkling fragments.
  - Crowbar chipping ice.
  - Bucket sand «шшш», 1 s.
  - Twig broom at 1.5 Hz.

### 8.8 Building and electrical

- **ТП transformer, 100 Hz:** sines at 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Hz with amplitudes 1 / 0.5 / 0.35 / 0.2 / 0.1, slow ±1 dB jitter. Sodium ballasts add a faint buzz.
- **Fluorescent ballast:** 100 Hz pulse/saw → bandpass 300–3000 Hz, plus lamination rattle (noise AM at 100 Hz).
- **Failing tube:** starter «тик-тик» (2–3 clicks per 0.3 s), a «тинк» ping (sine 4 kHz, 20 ms) on each strike, flashes every 0.5–3 s.

**Lift sequence:**
1. Call-button click.
2. **Relay cascade** in the machine room, carrying down the shaft: 4–12 clicks (2 ms impulse → bandpass 2–5 kHz) in clusters 10–80 ms apart.
3. Brake solenoid «клац-бум» ("clack-boom"): 70 Hz thump plus 1.5 kHz clack, 80 ms.
4. Motor hum at 50 / 100 / 150 Hz plus a rising 550–700 Hz whine at −18 dB. Through concrete, lowpass at 400 Hz.
5. Travel:
   - cab rumble (lowpass 200 Hz noise);
   - guide-shoe «тук» at rail joints every 1–2 s;
   - a landing-switch «тук» every floor (≈2.75 s);
   - cable and rail rattle and shaft wind hiss;
   - a «вжух» ("whoosh") mid-shaft as the counterweight passes.
6. Slowdown: the 2-speed motor drops ≈4× in pitch, then a stop «бух» ("thump").
7. Door operator whir (saw 150 → 250 Hz, up to 400 Hz, 1.5 s), slide rumble, end-clang with modes at 180 / 410 / 760 Hz.
8. The doors wait 4–6 s, then close with a bang.

Throughout: the cab light buzzes. Overload buzzer ≈1 kHz continuous. **No "ding"** in 2008 stock; a modernised lift may have a single 880 Hz sine gong with 1.2 s decay.

**Intercom (stylised):**
- Key beep: square ≈2.2 kHz (range 1.2–2.2 kHz), 60 ms.
- Call: trill alternating 1.2/1.6 kHz at 10 Hz for 0.8 s, then a 0.6 s gap, repeating up to 30 s.
- Key accepted: 1047 / 1319 / 1568 Hz, 90 ms each.
- Lock released: «пи-пи-пи» ("beep-beep-beep") ≈2 kHz at 5 Hz for 3–7 s.
- Wrong key: 400 Hz buzz, 0.5 s.
- Voice through the panel: bandpass 300–3400 Hz, clipped, with 50 Hz hum.

**Doors:**
- **Magnetic lock** (300–600 kgf): release is a soft «тук» and the door springs ajar; re-engage is a metallic **«КЛАК»** (1–3 kHz plus a 70 Hz boom).
- **Steel entrance door slam** (60–80 kg): 5 ms noise impulse into modes at 70 / 140 / 230 / 390 / 620 / 1040 Hz, decaying 0.4–1.2 s, plus a 30 ms handle rattle and the vestibule (тамбур) reverb.

**Radiators:**
- Air gurgle: rising sine chirps 300 → 1200 Hz, 10–30 ms, 2–10/s in bursts.
- Expansion ticks: 1 ms clicks bandpassed at 3 kHz.
- Neighbour banging the riser with a key: 3–5 hits at 4 Hz, each ringing 800–2000 Hz with 1 s decay, heard on several floors.
- Riser flush: lowpass 1 kHz noise swell, 4–8 s.

**Garbage chute:**
- Hopper hinge squeal: 1–3 kHz chirp, 0.3 s, then a clang.
- The load falls 9–17 floors: impacts at 10–25/s for 2–4 s, cutoff falling 3 kHz → 500 Hz, level dropping.
- Final 70 Hz thump; occasionally broken glass.

**Through-wall life** (14–18 cm reinforced-concrete panels, TL ≈50 dB at mid frequencies):
- Wall filter: two cascaded lowpasses at 300 Hz, ≈−40 dB.
- **TV news:**
  - Male anchor f0 110–150 Hz, ≈5 syllables/s, 2–4 s phrases with 0.3–0.6 s pauses.
  - Declarative fall of 5–8 semitones at phrase end (IK-1), small rises at commas.
  - Synthesis: saw → formants 500 / 1500 / 2500 Hz, gated by a syllable envelope.
  - 21:00: «Час» on «Канал Один» with a fanfare in the style of Sviridov. Comedy shows add laugh-track bursts.
- **Other neighbours:**
  - Chair scrape: 80–300 Hz, 0.7 s.
  - Footsteps upstairs: 40–100 Hz thuds at 1.8 Hz.
  - Cistern refill hiss: 40 s.
  - Hammer drill (перфоратор), 09:00–21:00 only: ≈40 Hz impacts plus 1.2 kHz whine, bursts of 5–20 s through the structure.
- **Stairwell smokers' babble:** band-limited formant babble. Phrases:
  - «Ну давай, пока» ("OK, bye")
  - «Ты где?» ("Where are you?")
  - «Я уже подхожу» ("I'm almost there")
  - «Хлеба купил?» ("Did you buy bread?")
  - «Осторожно, скользко!» ("Careful, slippery!")
  - «Опять лифт не работает» ("The lift's broken again")
- **Plastic carrier bags:** crinkle grains at 2–8 kHz.

### 8.9 Outdoor human activity and commerce

- **Kids sledding** (17:00–20:00, loudest 1–10 January):
  - Plastic «ледянка» (sliding mat): bandpass 500–3000 Hz scrape, 2–4 s, fading. Runner sled on asphalt: a screech.
  - Voices: f0 250–400 Hz, /a/ formants F1 ≈1000 Hz, F2 ≈1700 Hz.
  - Lines: «А-а-а!» ("Aaah!"), «Мама, смотри!» ("Mum, look!"), «Моя очередь!» ("My turn!"), «Ещё разок!» ("One more time!"), «Домой!» ("Home!", a parent calling).
- **January petards («Корсак»):** impulse plus broadband noise with 3–6 flutter echoes between blocks. Rockets whistle up 1 → 3 kHz over 1.5 s.
- **Shop door IR chime «дин-дон»:** E5 → C5 (659/523 Hz), pure sine, 0.8 s decay. Same notes as the metro chime.
- **Shops:**
  - Dot-matrix receipt screech ≈1 s; scale beep; coins on a plastic tray.
  - Freezer compressor hum; 100 Hz fluorescent hum; the air-curtain fan drone.
- **Payment terminal:** 1 kHz touch beep, bill-acceptor whirr, thermal-printer buzz.
- **Payphone (Russia uses 425 Hz):** dial tone continuous; busy 0.4 s on / 0.4 s off; ringback 0.8 s on / 3.2 s off.
- **Underpass:** RT60 1.2–1.8 s. Wet footsteps on stone, an accordion or guitar busker, kiosk radio pop, dripping water.

## 10. Liminal design & anomaly ideas

### 10.1 Principles

1. **Traces, not absence.** Show lit windows, warm car hoods with melted roof snow, a cigarette smouldering in a bin, a sled leaning by a door. Empty, but *recently* occupied.
2. **Teach the baseline, then deviate** (the Exit 8 principle). Repeating courtyards make tiny differences readable.
3. **Keep everything deniable.** Each oddity has a mundane explanation the player can almost reach. Never confirm it, never escalate to a threat.
4. **Real scale.** Slabs stand 60–150 m apart, and real walking time makes the district feel endless.
5. **Pools of light with dark intervals.** Use slow eye adaptation (time constant 5–10 s), so a dark stretch gradually "opens up". It feels subtly unnatural.
6. **Warm inside, cold outside.** This is the doomer core: «окна чужих квартир» ("windows of other people's flats"), lit and unreachable. The kiosk is the last warm point; a lone smoker stands on a transit balcony.
7. **Audio does half the work.** Distant life only: a lift thump inside a building, a TV through a wall, dogs, an alarm three yards away, an elektrichka horn. No stingers and almost no music.
8. **Micro-motion against stillness:** a bag in a birch, a swing creak, a notice flapping on a door.
9. **Low fidelity evokes memory.** Aim at 2008 compact cameras and phones (2–3 MP, CCD vertical smear from lamps, chroma noise, purple fringing) rather than photorealism.
10. **No anachronism** (§0.3).

## 11. Rendering techniques & GLSL notes

### 11.1 Pipeline

1. Render the scene to a `HalfFloatType` target with a `DepthTexture`.
2. Full-screen **atmosphere pass**: height fog plus in-scattering from the nearest 16 lamps, picked on the CPU each frame.
3. Snow points, with their own fog.
4. Bloom.
5. `OutputPass` with `AgXToneMapping` (or ACES), exposure ≈1.0.
6. Final pass: grade, grain, dither.

Every intermediate target must be `HalfFloatType`, or the dark gradients will band.

### 11.2 Analytic lamp in-scattering (closed form plus equiangular cone sampling)

**Setup:** ray x(t) = o + d·t of length D; lamp position p; q = o − p; b = d·q; h = √(q·q − b²).

**Closed form** (isotropic point light, inverse square, no extinction):

∫₀^D dt / |x(t) − p|² = [atan((D + b)/h) − atan(b/h)] / h

Substituting t = h·tan φ − b makes the integrand uniform in φ (equiangular sampling, Kulla & Fajardo 2012). Then 3–4 blue-noise-jittered samples are enough to add the cone, extinction and phase.

```glsl
uniform float uSigma;               // 0.012–0.02 /m
float phaseHG(float c, float g){ float g2=g*g; return (1.-g2)/(12.566*pow(1.+g2-2.*g*c,1.5)); }

vec3 lampInscatter(vec3 ro, vec3 rd, float D, vec3 lp, vec3 Lcol, float cosOuter, float jit){
  vec3 q = ro - lp;
  float b = dot(rd, q);
  float h = sqrt(max(dot(q,q) - b*b, 1e-4));
  float p0 = atan(b/h), p1 = atan((D + b)/h);
  float acc = 0.;
  for (int i = 0; i < 4; i++){
    float t = h*tan(mix(p0, p1, (float(i)+jit)*0.25)) - b;
    vec3 x = ro + rd*t, v = x - lp; float r = length(v); v /= r;
    float cone = smoothstep(cosOuter, cosOuter+0.25, -v.y) + 0.03;   // faint glare above head
    acc += cone * phaseHG(dot(v, -rd), 0.4) * exp(-uSigma*(t + r));  // view + light extinction
  }
  return Lcol * uSigma * acc * (p1 - p0) / (h * 4.0);
}
```

- Street heads: `cosOuter` ≈0.35 (≈140° full cone).
- **Reconstructing D in the pass:**
  1. `ndc = vec4(uv*2.-1., depth*2.-1., 1.)`
  2. `vp = projInv*ndc; vp /= vp.w`
  3. `D = length(vp.xyz)`
  4. `rd = mat3(camWorld)*normalize(vp.xyz)`
  5. For sky pixels, D = 400.
- **Cost:** 16 lamps × 4 samples. Run at half resolution on integrated GPUs.
- The orange halos should come from this pass; keep bloom subtle.

### 11.3 Exponential height fog

**Density:** σ(y) = σ₀ + a·e^(−b(y − y_g)), where y_g is ground height.

**Optical depth** for a ray starting at height y₀:

τ = σ₀D + a·e^(−b(y₀ − y_g))·(1 − e^(−b·d_y·D))/(b·d_y)

The last factor becomes D when d_y ≈ 0.

```glsl
float opticalDepth(vec3 ro, vec3 rd, float D){
  float k = uFogFall*rd.y;
  float f = abs(k) < 1e-4 ? D : (1. - exp(-k*D))/k;
  return uSigma0*D + uFogA*exp(-uFogFall*(ro.y - uGround))*f;
}
// col = col*exp(-tau) + fogCol*(1.-exp(-tau)) + Σ lampInscatter(...)
```

- **Values:** σ₀ = 0.006–0.015; a = 0.03–0.06; b = 0.08–0.2 /m (ground mist 5–12 m thick).
- **fogCol** ≈ 0.8 × the horizon sky colour (e.g. `#4A3E44`). Never grey-white, or the fog turns milky.
- Blend sky and fog at the horizon so the far plane disappears.

### 11.4 Grade, grain, dither

```glsl
// linear, before encode: Purkinje-style night desaturation + lifted blue black
float l = dot(c, vec3(.2126,.7152,.0722));
c = mix(c, l*vec3(.62,.72,1.)*1.15, (1.-smoothstep(.02,.25,l))*.55);
c = c*.97 + vec3(.006,.006,.010);
// LAST op, after sRGB encode: triangular blue-noise dither (±1 LSB)
vec3 n = texture(tBlueNoise, (gl_FragCoord.xy + vec2(17,29)*uFrame)/64.).rgb*2.-1.;
n = sign(n)*(1. - sqrt(1. - abs(n)));             // uniform → triangular PDF
c += n/255.;
// fallback: interleaved gradient noise (Jimenez 2014)
float ign(vec2 p){ return fract(52.9829189*fract(dot(p, vec2(.06711056,.00583715)))); }
```

- Blue noise: Christoph Peters' free 64 × 64 textures (CC0).
- Optional retro posterisation: Bayer 4 × 4 `[0,8,2,10,12,4,14,6,3,11,1,9,15,7,13,5]/16`, then `floor(c*levels + bayer)/levels` with levels = 32–64.
- Film grain: 2–3% luminance noise, animated per frame.
- Set `material.dithering = true` on lit materials.
- Eye adaptation time constant: 5–10 s (§10.1).

### 11.5 Bloom

```js
renderer.toneMapping = THREE.AgXToneMapping;
const bloom = new UnrealBloomPass(new THREE.Vector2(w/2, h/2), 0.3 /*strength*/, 0.6 /*radius*/, 1.0 /*threshold, linear HDR*/);
```

- **Emissive levels (linear):** lamp heads 6–10; lit windows 1.5–3; snow under lamps 0.6–0.9 (below threshold, so snow never blooms).
- Alternative photographic halation with no threshold: `final = mix(scene, blurredScene, 0.04)`.

### 11.6 GPU snow (camera-relative wrap, lamp-lit)

- **Layers** of `THREE.Points`:
  - Near: 12 m box, 15k flakes, ≈6 mm flakes.
  - Far: 50 m box, 30k flakes.
  - Optional third layer: screen-space noise for density.
- **Material:** additive blending, `depthWrite: false`, depth test on.
- Include `phaseHG` from §11.2.

```glsl
attribute vec4 aSeed;                    // xyz ∈[0,1), w random
uniform float uTime, uBox, uPx;          // uPx = viewportH / (2*tan(fov/2))
uniform vec3 uCam, uWind, uAmbient;      // ambient ≈ skyglow*0.05
uniform vec4 uLamp[8]; uniform vec3 uLampCol[8];
varying vec3 vCol; varying float vA;
void main(){
  float r = aSeed.w;
  vec3 p = aSeed.xyz*uBox + uWind*uTime;
  p.y -= mix(0.6, 1.3, r)*uTime;                                         // m/s
  p.xz += 0.25*vec2(sin(uTime*(1.1+r)+r*40.), cos(uTime*(0.8+r)+r*90.)); // flutter
  p = uCam + mod(p - uCam + 0.5*uBox, uBox) - 0.5*uBox;                  // wrap around camera
  vec3 V = normalize(uCam - p), L = uAmbient;
  for (int i = 0; i < 8; i++){
    vec3 d = p - uLamp[i].xyz; float r2 = dot(d,d); vec3 v = d*inversesqrt(r2);
    L += uLampCol[i]*uLamp[i].w*smoothstep(.35,.65,-v.y)*phaseHG(dot(v,V), .55)/r2; // forward glow
  }
  vec4 mv = viewMatrix*vec4(p,1.); float z = -mv.z;
  float s = 0.006*mix(.7,1.4,r)*uPx/z;
  gl_PointSize = clamp(s, 1., 8.);
  vA = (1.-smoothstep(.35*uBox, .5*uBox, length(p-uCam))) * smoothstep(.3,1.,z) * min(s,1.);
  vCol = L; gl_Position = projectionMatrix*mv;
}
// frag: a = vA*(1.-smoothstep(.2,.5,length(gl_PointCoord-.5))); out = vec4(vCol*a, a);
```

- Sub-pixel flakes get dimmer, not smaller (`min(s,1.)`), to avoid shimmer.
- Keep `uTime` below ≈1e4 s; reset it during scene transitions.
- **Frost nights:** light pillars as additive world-up billboards above bright lamps, 10–30 m tall, with a vertical fade.

### 11.7 Voxel AO with greedy meshing (0fps / Lysenko method)

```js
// per face vertex: two edge neighbours s1,s2 and the corner c, on the face's outer layer
const ao = (s1 && s2) ? 0 : 3 - (s1 + s2 + c);      // 0 darkest … 3 open
const AO = [0.40, 0.62, 0.82, 1.0];
if (a00 + a11 > a01 + a10) emitFlippedQuad(); else emitQuad();  // fixes anisotropic interpolation
// greedy mask key: merge only identical keys
const key = mat | (a0 << 8) | (a1 << 10) | (a2 << 12) | (a3 << 14);
```

At 0.25 m, AO is very local (window reveals, balcony undersides, panel joints), so add two larger-scale terms:
- **Sky visibility,** baked per vertex: cast 8–16 rays through a 1 m occupancy grid up to 20 m, in a worker thread.
- **Snow bounce:** `bounce = 0.8 * groundLampIrradiance * 0.3 * exp(-heightAboveGround/2.0)`. This makes the lower 2–3 floors glow orange from snow under sodium lamps, which matches reality.

### 11.8 Procedural lit windows

Facade quads carry facade-local `uv` (metres along the wall and upward) and a `wallId`.

```glsl
float h21(vec2 p){ p = fract(p*vec2(123.34,456.21)); p += dot(p,p+45.32); return fract(p.x*p.y); }
vec3 windowLight(vec2 uv, float wallId, float hour, float time){
  const vec2 BAY = vec2(3.0, 2.75);
  vec2 cell = floor(uv/BAY), f = fract(uv/BAY)*BAY;
  vec2 lo = vec2(.75,.85), hi = vec2(2.25,2.35);                      // 1.5×1.5 m, sill 0.85
  float glass = step(lo.x,f.x)*step(f.x,hi.x)*step(lo.y,f.y)*step(f.y,hi.y);
  float hA = h21(vec2(floor(cell.x/2.), cell.y) + wallId*7.31);        // apartment (2 bays)
  float hR = h21(cell + wallId*3.17 + .5);                              // room
  float t = hour < 12. ? hour + 24. : hour;                             // 12..36
  float eve  = step(16.5 + 5.*hR, t)*step(t, 22. + 5.*hA*hA + hR)*step(.12, hA); // 12% empty flats
  float morn = step(30., t)*step(t, 32.5 + hR)*step(.55, hR);           // 06:00–08:30
  float k = h21(cell.yx + wallId);
  vec3 c = k<.70 ? vec3(1.,.55,.20) : k<.82 ? vec3(1.,.80,.55) : k<.90 ? vec3(.85,.30,.12)
         : vec3(.35,.45,1.)*(.4+.6*h21(vec2(floor(time*.7+k*10.), k)));  // TV "scene cuts"
  vec2 w = (f-lo)/(hi-lo);
  float vig = smoothstep(0.,.25,w.x)*smoothstep(1.,.75,w.x)*mix(.7,1.,w.y); // curtain falloff
  return c*max(eve,morn)*vig*mix(1.2,3.0,hR)*glass;
}
```

- Validate the output against the schedule in §1.5.
- **Facade layouts:** store each series' bay pattern (kitchen, room, stair, loggia) in a small 1D lookup texture indexed by `cell.x % len`.
- **Stair column:** always lit, with a half-floor vertical offset in 9-storey series.
- **Distance anti-aliasing:** `a = smoothstep(.3, 1., max(fwidth(uv.x), fwidth(uv.y)))`, then blend toward `avgColor * litFraction * 0.27` (the glass-area fraction).
- **Dark glass:** sky colour at the reflected direction × Fresnel (≈0.04–0.1).
- **Frost (optional):** `frost = smoothstep(.35,.75, fbm(w*6.)*.6 + (1.-w.y)*.5)`. Frost brightens and blurs the interior light.
- **Garlands:** a separate hash gate at 5–10% of windows, active 20 Dec – 14 Jan, chasing at 1–2 Hz.

### 11.9 Shadows

- **Bake static lamps.** Lamps never move, so at mesh time sum the lamp contributions with visibility:
  - 3D-DDA (Amanatides & Woo) through the voxel grid.
  - Store the result in a **1 m light volume** (RGBA8 3D texture), sampled at `p + N*0.5`. This avoids splitting greedy quads.
- **Player capsule shadow**, the long shadow that swings past each lamp:

```glsl
float playerShadow(vec3 wp, vec3 F, vec3 lp){            // F = feet; ground fragment wp
  vec3 H = F + vec3(0., 1.7, 0.);
  vec2 S = lp.xz + (H.xz - lp.xz)*((lp.y - F.y)/(lp.y - H.y)); // head projected to ground
  vec2 pa = wp.xz - F.xz, ba = S - F.xz;
  float u = clamp(dot(pa,ba)/dot(ba,ba), 0., 1.), d = length(pa - ba*u), w = mix(.18,.35,u);
  return mix(.25, 1., smoothstep(w*.6, w, d));            // multiply that lamp's term only
}
```

- **Screen-space contact shadows:** march 12 steps over 0.6 m toward the light in view space. A step is occluded when `0.02 < sceneZ − rayZ < 0.3`. Use for props under the nearest 1–2 lamps.
- **Cars and benches:** a soft dark quad underneath, offset away from the dominant lamp.

### 11.10 Footprints

- **Decals:** a `THREE.InstancedMesh` ring buffer of 512 instances.
  - Plane 0.12 × 0.30 m, `polygonOffset` (factor −1, units −4), `depthWrite: false`.
  - One print per 0.72 m of travel, alternating ±0.09 m off the path.
  - Spawn time in an instanced attribute `aBorn`.
  - Alpha: `1 − smoothstep(fadeStart, fadeEnd, uTime − aBorn)`.
- **Fade timing:**
  - While snowing: fadeEnd ≈300–600 s. Real fill rate is 1–2 cm/h for prints 3–6 cm deep, compressed for the game.
  - **Without snowfall prints never fade,** which enables anomaly 10.
- **Look:** a normal map, with the interior darker and bluer (§1.3 colours) and a slightly brighter rim.
- **Alternative:** a top-down trail render target (1024² over 64 m, ≈6 cm per texel) with toroidal addressing around the player. Stamp prints into it, decay with a ping-pong pass, and sample it in the snow shader for darkening plus a normal offset.
- **Worn paths (тропинки)** are pre-baked into the ground texture.

### 11.11 Extras

- **Snow glints:**
  - Hash 2.5 cm cells (`floor(wp*40.)`) and keep ≈15%.
  - Perturb the normal by the hash and use `pow(max(dot(reflect(-L, n), V), 0.), 400.)`.
  - Fade beyond 15 m. The sparkles flicker as the camera moves.
- **CCD smear** (optional "phone camera" mode): for each bright lamp on screen, an additive 1–2 px vertical line the full screen height.
- **Branch shadows:** project a procedural branch mask from facade floodlights, swaying at ≈0.3 Hz.
- **Sodium warm-up:** animate emissive from `#C86A8A` to `#FFA64A` over 3–5 min at switch-on (≈15–25 min after sunset). Give dying lamps a cycling state machine.
