# Dead Center (Left 4 Dead 2, Campaign 1): Research Brief for a Level Rebuild

Prepared for a private, non-published PS1-style fan recreation. Everything below is paraphrased from community wikis, guides and game data, plus cross-checking between those sources. The original game randomizes item spawns, Special Infected, Witches and Tanks through the "AI Director". Treat any spawn location as a possible spot, not a guaranteed one, unless it is marked as fixed.

Notation:
- **(?)** means uncertain, unverified, or reconstructed from memory without a second source.
- **(est.)** means an approximate size or time estimate made for blockout purposes. These are not measured from the original map files.
- Directions such as "left/right" are given from the player's point of view while moving forward along the main route.

---

## 0. Campaign Overview and Story Arc

### Setting
- **Place:** Savannah, Georgia (US Deep South), during the first days after the Green Flu reaches the city. The eastern US has already fallen; Savannah is only just being hit.
- **Time of year:** Early autumn. In-world posters advertise a mall "grand reopening" appearance by race driver Jimmy Gibbs Jr. on **October 14, 4–8 pm**, and the mall renovation was due to finish in "Fall 2009". The weather is warm and humid.
- **Time of day:** Daytime throughout, with a hazy, warm, afternoon light (?). The light is golden or orange, and smoke haze from the burning hotel and city fires dulls the sky. It never rains in this campaign.
- **Authority presence:** CEDA (the fictional federal emergency agency) had turned the hotel, and then the mall, into evacuation centres. Both have now been abandoned or overrun. CEDA gear is everywhere: decontamination trailers, tents, cots, IV stands, solar-powered road message signs pointing evacuees to the mall, posters and checklists. In the first two chapters you also meet CEDA hazmat-suit infected.

### The four survivors
The survivors are strangers who meet on the hotel roof.
- **Coach:** a heavy-set local high-school coach.
- **Ellis:** a young local mechanic who is chatty and loves stock-car racing.
- **Nick:** a cynical gambler in a white suit.
- **Rochelle:** a TV news producer who was covering the evacuation.

They introduce themselves for the first time inside the hotel elevator in chapter 1. In the first chapters they don't yet know the "official" names of the Special Infected, so they invent nicknames for them.

### Story beats
1. **Hotel:** The survivors reach the roof of "The Vannah" hotel, the CEDA evacuation point, just as the last helicopter leaves without them. The hotel below them is on fire. They decide to follow the helicopter to the other evac centre at Liberty Mall across town. They fight down through two burning guest floors, ride the one working elevator to the ground floor, and get out through the burning kitchen, restaurant and lobby to a safe room. On the way, a CEDA wall map shows that almost the whole country is lost and that New Orleans is the last refuge.
2. **Streets:** The direct bridge or overpass to the mall is barricaded, so they detour through buildings, under a freeway and over a pedestrian walkway. Ellis or Coach knows a gun shop on the way. The owner, Whitaker, has barricaded himself on the roof. He trades the guns, plus clearing a fuel tanker that blocks the road to the mall, for a six-pack of cola from the alarmed grocery store next door. The survivors fetch it, he blows up the tanker, and they reach the mall entrance.
3. **Mall:** Liberty Mall is dark and overrun, and CEDA has gone. The survivors cross the department store and the mall floors, then set off a security alarm (either a toy-store window or an emergency door). They run to the top-floor security office to switch it off, and continue through a half-built renovation area and a deserted CEDA post to a safe room.
4. **Atrium (finale):** From the top floor they see the central atrium. A famous local driver's stock car stands on display in the middle of the ground floor. While riding a glass elevator down, Ellis proposes refuelling the car. The survivors collect gas cans around the multi-level atrium, fill the car, and crash it out through the mall's front doors, heading for New Orleans.

### Opening cinematic gist (game intro, before chapter 1)
The general L4D2 intro movie cuts between several campaigns. Its last section belongs to Dead Center:
- The four survivors climb a hotel stairwell. Coach is out of breath and complains about an evac station "thirty floors up", and Nick makes a joke.
- They push out onto the roof and call out, but nobody answers.
- Several helicopters are already flying away. Rochelle is in disbelief, and Coach angrily asks why the people who were supposed to save them have left.

In the actual level the hotel is much shorter (the wiki says 6 to 8 storeys). The intro shows many flights of stairs, but the playable building does not.

### Loading screens and transitions
- **Loading screen:** The campaign's movie-style poster is shown pinned on a wall beside other campaign posters, with a loading bar. There is no narrative text.
  - The poster shows the four survivors holding only melee weapons, one of them with a chainsaw.
  - Its tagline is a pun about prices getting "slashed", fitting the mall theme. Write your own version.
- **Chapter titles:** 1 "Hotel", 2 "Streets", 3 "Mall", 4 "Atrium". Wikis sometimes call them "The Hotel" and "Atrium Finale".
- **Between chapters:** Each chapter ends when all living survivors are inside the safe room with the door closed. The game then shows a stats or "chapter complete" summary. The next chapter starts in a matching safe room, which is the same room seen from inside.
- **End of campaign:** The finale cutscene plays, then end credits list the players with their stats. Dead Center's cutscene is the only finale that ends on a freeze-frame of the vehicle mid-jump.

### Global atmosphere guide
- **Colour palette:** Warm and dusty: ochre, burnt orange, brick red, olive and beige. CEDA hazmat yellow and green, and plywood tan, add accents. The hotel interior is lit orange by fire and grey by smoke. The streets are sun-bleached concrete and asphalt, with green hedges and lawns and Southern trees. The mall has dim 1980s tones of teal, mauve and beige tile, with grey construction plastic. The atrium is bright from its skylights.
- **Weather and sky:** Clear but hazy, with smoke columns from the city. There is no rain or fog, apart from smoke indoors.
- **Ambient sound:**
  - Hotel: crackling fire, roaring flare-ups, and fire-alarm strobes that flash silently (there is no siren in the original).
  - Throughout: distant infected moans and screams, and the departing helicopter at the start, which is very loud in the original.
  - Streets: car alarms (on alarmed cars) and the shrill grocery-store burglar alarm.
  - Mall: a ringing mall security alarm and echoing interiors.
  - Finale: the music rises at the start and when the car is ready.
  - Each campaign in L4D2 has its own styled horde music (?).
- **Recurring visual storytelling:**
  - Barricades: furniture against hotel doors, vending machines blocking mall doors with floor jacks next to them, and plywood over windows put up by CEDA or staff.
  - Piles of bodies, blood drag-trails and dead CEDA workers.
  - Sleeping bags and makeshift blanket shelters.
  - "Sale" and "store closing" signs in the mall.
  - Repeated billboards, banners and posters advertising the stock-car star's appearance at the mall. These lead the player toward the finale.

### Gameplay systems that shape the layouts
- **Weapon tiers:**
  - Survivors start with one pistol. A second pistol makes dual pistols; a Magnum can replace them.
  - Tier 1 weapons are SMG, silenced SMG, pump shotgun and chrome shotgun.
  - Tier 2 weapons are assault rifle, AK, combat (burst) rifle, auto shotguns, hunting and sniper rifles.
  - "Special" pickups are the grenade launcher and the M60 (M60 not in Dead Center (?)).
  - Melee weapons can be found in Dead Center: fire axe, crowbar, cricket bat, baseball bat, katana, knife, golf club (added later on PC) and chainsaw. There is no machete, frying pan, guitar or nightstick in this campaign.
- **Throwables and upgrades:** Throwables are pipe bomb, Molotov and bile jar. Upgrades are the laser sight and incendiary or explosive ammo packs. Dead Center is the one campaign that guarantees a laser sight (in the gun shop).
- **Healing:** First-aid kits, pills, adrenaline and defibrillators. Every safe room normally has 4 first-aid kits and an ammo pile.
- **Rescue closets:** A dead survivor can reappear locked in a small closet later in the level, and must be let out by teammates.
- **Uncommon infected:** CEDA hazmat workers appear only in chapters 1–2. They are fireproof and sometimes drop a bile jar. A very rare zombified Jimmy Gibbs Jr. can appear in the finale.
- **Crescendo types:**
  - "Hold-out": wait until the horde stops, for example at the hotel elevator.
  - "Gauntlet": an alarm keeps calling hordes until you run to an off switch or reach a goal (grocery and mall alarms).
  - "Scavenge finale": collect cans under waves of infected (the atrium).
- **Boss rules in campaign mode:** No Witch or Tank appears in chapter 1 in co-op. Bosses are introduced in chapter 2.

## 1. Chapter 1: "Hotel" (map c1m1_hotel)

### At a glance
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Start | Roof of the hotel (about 8 storeys high (?)) |
| End | Safe room off the ground-floor lobby, near the front entrance |
| Vertical route | Roof → stairs → **8th floor** → stairs → **7th floor** (burning) → elevator → **ground floor** (burning) |
| Crescendo | Elevator arrival (hold-out horde, with fire as a hazard) |
| Bosses (co-op) | None. A Tank is possible only in competitive mode, after the elevator |
| Typical length | Short. Walking path about 350–450 m (est.) |
| Typical time | 5–8 min (casual co-op, Normal) (est.) |

The floor numbers come from room numbers on the burning floor (708 and 711) and a reference to the 8th floor above it. The wiki gives the building's height inconsistently, as 6 or 8 storeys.

### 1.1 Opening situation (rooftop)
- **Intro shot:** A helicopter lifts off and leaves. Coach shouts for it to come back, and someone calls out that there are still people up here. The helicopter dips away out of view. One survivor reasons that it is heading to the mall evacuation centre across town and offers to lead them there. The group agrees to "grab a weapon and go". Someone remarks that they don't like the sounds coming from downstairs.
- **Roof layout (est.):**
  - The flat roof is about 30 × 40 m, with a raised parapet and the evac helipad, which is now empty.
  - There are HVAC units, vents, CEDA equipment and possibly a few tents, crates and chairs (?).
  - A small brick or concrete stair housing with a metal door leads inside.
  - The Savannah skyline and smoke columns are visible all around.
  - Coach starts near the roof edge, and Nick is just coming out of the roof door.
- **Starting items:** A table near the roof door holds:
  - 4 first-aid kits;
  - extra pistols (so survivors can go dual-wield);
  - 1–3 melee weapons (axe, crowbar or similar).
  - There are no Tier 1 guns yet. The team's choice is pistols or melee.

### 1.2 Roof stairwell
- One flight of stairs (two half-flights with a landing) leads down inside the stair housing to a door. Some infected are usually standing just behind this door.
- **Mood:** A dim concrete stairwell with emergency lighting.

### 1.3 Eighth floor: CEDA command floor and the ledge walk
This is a typical hotel guest corridor: carpet, wallpaper, numbered doors, sconce lights and fire-alarm strobes. The light is smoky, with daylight coming in through open rooms.

Route in order:
1. **Entry corridor:** You exit the stair door into a long straight corridor, about 2.5 m wide, 3 m high and 25–35 m long (est.), with guest-room doors on both sides. A side door on the left can be closed to stop infected coming through. Some guest rooms have furniture half-dragged toward the doors, like unfinished barricades. There are bodies, blood, and a dead infected next to a room-service phone.
2. **CEDA "Suite A":** The first double doors on the right lead to the meeting or command room.
   - It contains a long conference table covered with maps.
   - A large US wall map shows that the whole East Coast and the North-East have been lost, and marks the outbreak's origin near Philadelphia. The survivors comment that New Orleans looks like the last place standing.
   - A poster board explains the Boomer's anatomy, with a photo of one in a cage.
   - A connecting door leads to a suite bedroom. Its nightstand is a common Magnum spot.
   - A balcony looks down at the five glass roof panels of the lobby wing below. In the original, falling through these panels was a known exploit.
3. **Further along the corridor:** You clear more guest rooms, which may hold pills, adrenaline and throwables. Balcony dividers between rooms are knocked down. A small supply or bathroom spot may hold a defibrillator (?).
4. **Corridor junction and elevator lobby:** The hallways cross here. There is a bank of elevators with doors shut and power out. The survivors say the elevator is out and they should take the stairs.
5. **Fire flare-up (scripted):**
   - Turn **left** at the junction. A pile of burning wreckage blocks the corridor ahead.
   - As you approach, it **bursts into a bigger blaze**, an explosive flare-up that blocks the corridor completely.
   - The survivors say the fire is spreading and that they can get around it by walking along the outside ledge.
6. **Small room:** Turn **right** into a small room that juts out of the building. Guides variously call it a laundry room, a supply closet or a storage room. It often holds supplies. Close its door to stop Smokers.
7. **Window break and ledge walk:**
   - Break the window and step out onto the exterior ledge, which is about 0.8–1 m wide and **7–8 floors above the ground** (fatal fall).
   - Walk along the facade past several room windows for about 15–25 m (est.). Daylight here is bright and warm, with the city visible below.
   - This is the classic danger spot: Chargers, Jockeys and Boomer blasts can knock survivors off, so they end up hanging or dead.
   - An optional jump down to the next floor's ledge is possible but risky.
8. **Re-entry:** Climb back in through the window of the **second room on the left** along the ledge. Some guides say "one of the next two rooms". This puts you past the fire, back in the corridor.
9. **Corridor to the stairwell:** Continue to the end of the corridor and the stairwell door.
   - The main stairs further on are blocked by burning furniture, so only this one stairwell leads down.
   - A **rescue closet** is in the room nearest the elevator on this floor.

### 1.4 Stairwell down to the seventh floor
- One flight down. The **bottom landing is on fire**: furniture that was stacked as a barricade is now burning. You open the door at the bottom into the next corridor.

### 1.5 Seventh floor: the burning floor
- **Mood:** Much heavier smoke and an orange glow. Fire spreads along the carpet. A knocked-over ironing board is shown as the cause of one fire. A broken sprinkler drips in one spot, but the sprinklers otherwise don't work.
- **Layout:** A corridor with about **four guest rooms**, two of which are burning (rooms 708 and 711 are named in sources). Fire-rated corridor doors may be passed along the way.
- **Smoke-under-door hazard (scripted):**
  - Doors with smoke curling out from underneath hide a fire.
  - Opening one makes a fireball burst out and spreads fire into the corridor. This works as a trap, or as a weapon against infected chasing you.
  - Infected can also claw these doors open.
  - If the corridor is jammed, use the side rooms and their balconies to bypass it.
- **Another laundry or utility room** is on this floor (optional).
- **Elevator lobby or lounge** at the end of the corridor:
  - A larger open room with sleeping bags on the floor, where evacuees slept, plus an ashtray stand and a closet (common grenade spot).
  - There are **two elevators**:
    - **one working**: doors open, lights on, and the survivors note that this one still has power;
    - **one with its doors closed**.
  - Balconies run outside the elevator lobby.
  - A room off the lounge on the right may have a Magnum on a desk by the bed, or a chainsaw.
  - A **rescue closet** is in the room nearest the elevator.
  - The survivors joke that you shouldn't use elevators during a fire.

### 1.6 The elevator ride (scripted event)
- **Trigger:** Every living survivor must be inside the car, and one of them presses the button. The doors close and the car starts down. It will not leave if someone incapacitated is still outside.
- **Ride length:** About 30–45 s (est.). This is the safe moment to heal.
- **Dialogue:** The introductions happen here.
  - One survivor panics about the "zombie apocalypse".
  - Coach calms him down and asks his name. Ellis gives his name and rambles a little, Rochelle introduces herself, and Nick introduces himself while saying he doesn't plan to stick around. Coach asks them to stay together.
  - There are different versions if a survivor has already died; for example, the others ask who they lost.
  - The scene ends with someone saying "enough chat, get ready".
- **Arrival:**
  - Smoke fills the car and the screen as it nears the bottom.
  - The **power dies** and the car stops at the ground floor with its doors jammed.
  - A survivor has to **pry the doors open**, which is noisy. This sets off the **chapter's crescendo**.
  - The ground-floor fires only "switch on" visually as the car gets close (an optimization trick in the original).

### 1.7 Ground floor: the crescendo
- **What you see when the doors open:** A smoke-filled back-of-house or service corridor area, with patches of fire everywhere on floors and furniture and an orange glow.
  - A **silenced SMG** lies on the floor right in front of the elevator. It is a multi-pickup spawn, so you can keep grabbing fresh guns.
  - This is the first Tier 1 gun of the campaign.
- **Horde behaviour:** A large horde rushes the elevator. Ordinary infected often run into the fires and burn to death. CEDA hazmat infected are fireproof and walk straight through.
  - Good tactics in the original: hold in the elevator doorway, or throw a bile jar into a fire so the horde burns itself.
- **Security office:** The first room on the right. It always has **3 chrome shotguns**, a **first-aid kit**, ammo (?) and CCTV monitors. It is a good place to hold during the horde.
- **Burning wreckage to the left:** Grenades and pills often spawn in the side rooms and around this wreckage.

### 1.8 Ground floor: kitchen, buffet, lobby and safe room
1. **Corridor** → **hotel kitchen:**
   - Ovens are left on and burning, with steel prep tables and shelves where throwables may be.
   - A row of bodies has been laid out deliberately, as if the kitchen was being used as a morgue.
   - You climb onto and over a **metal table or counter** to get through.
2. **Buffet or restaurant room:**
   - Large and full of fire. The safe path snakes around the burning areas.
   - A corner room on the far side is a good defensive pocket.
   - CEDA infected can cut straight through the flames to flank you.
3. **Lobby:**
   - A large, grand lobby, probably 2 storeys tall with a skylight (est.).
   - Pillars cast deep shadows where Special Infected like to wait.
   - The reception counter is on the **left side**.
   - Windows and doors are boarded with plywood, and piles of bodies lie around.
   - Many idle infected stand here; throwing a grenade in first is standard.
   - A sign in the lobby advertises free coffee and breakfast.
4. **A short, plain white hallway** leads to the **safe room** at the hotel's entrance side.
   - The safe room is smoke-free at first, but smoke slowly drifts in if the door is left open.
   - A medical-transport timetable is pinned on the wall, mostly blank.

### 1.9 Item and threat summary for chapter 1
- **Fixed items:**
  - Roof: 4 first-aid kits, pistols and melee.
  - Ground floor: silenced SMG in front of the elevator.
  - Security room: 3 chrome shotguns and a first-aid kit.
  - Safe room: standard medkits and ammo (?).
- **Random items:**
  - Magnum: Suite A bedroom nightstand, or the 7th-floor room by the lounge.
  - Chainsaw: 7th-floor room.
  - Pills, adrenaline and defibrillators: closets, bathrooms and the ledge-end rooms.
  - Pipe bombs and bile jars: the elevator-lobby closet, kitchen shelves and wreckage spots.
  - CEDA infected may drop bile jars.
- **Specials:**
  - Smokers in rooms and on the ledge (one can pull from the ledge below).
  - Chargers and Jockeys in the long corridors and on the ledge.
  - Boomers anywhere.
  - In the original, specials sometimes don't spawn on the lower floors until a survivor steps onto a particular balcony railing.
- **Bosses:** No Witch or Tank in co-op.

## 2. Chapter 2: "Streets" (map c1m2_streets)

### At a glance
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Start | Safe room by the hotel entrance, opening onto the hotel's covered drive |
| End | Safe room at the mall entrance (back entrance of the department store) beside a CEDA-used parking lot |
| Crescendo | Grocery-store alarm (gauntlet): fetch the cola and deliver it to the gun-shop owner's rooftop door |
| Bosses | First Witch and first Tank of the campaign (random). A Tank in this map is very likely in co-op |
| Typical length | The longest of the four. Walking path about 900–1300 m (est.) |
| Typical time | 12–20 min (casual co-op, Normal) (est.) |

### 2.1 Opening situation (safe room)
- **Contents:** Tier 1 guns on a counter (SMG and shotgun types), 4 first-aid kits and an ammo pile on a table. A melee weapon may be near the right-hand wall (facing the exit) or on the floor of a small back room. There are also 2 sleeping bags, empty water jugs and CEDA posters saying that only registered or tracked evacuees will be helped. A small barricade sits by the inner door.
- **Dialogue gist:** One survivor (Ellis or Coach) says they know a gun store on the way to the mall where they can get real weapons. Nick makes a sarcastic remark about Savannah, Coach objects to his attitude, and Rochelle tells Nick to drop the act.

### 2.2 Hotel forecourt and the sloped street
1. **Exit the safe room and turn right** under the hotel's **covered entrance canopy** (porte-cochère). It is shadowy, and infected lurk in the shade.
   - Corpses are piled outside each lobby entry door.
   - A **bus** is parked nearby. A **green awning** near the back of the bus often has a gas can or propane tank under it.
2. **Two small side options:**
   - **Left:** a **CEDA trailer** next to the hedges, with a table beside it (a grenade or ammo-upgrade spot, plus a gas can or propane).
   - **Right:** a stretch of fence with pills or a grenade at its base.
3. **The slope:** A **large sloped street** climbs ahead.
   - It has a **planted centre median** with bushes where infected hide.
   - **Barricades** line the right-hand side. The safest line is to keep to the far-right wall.
   - Hordes come from both directions.
   - A street sign bans glass bottles, but empty beer packs lie in the road.

### 2.3 Upper street: the CEDA checkpoint
- **At the top of the slope:**
  - a **police car**;
  - **CEDA tents**, including a medical tent with IV stands, "contaminated" blood bags and a dead CEDA worker (common grenade launcher or chainsaw spot);
  - a **pickup truck**, with bodies of hazmat workers it seems to have run over, and a weapon, a grenade and propane in its tray;
  - bushes;
  - a **CEDA solar message sign** pointing the way to the mall.
  - Possibly also an ammo-upgrade spot on the left near the police car.
- **Billboards:** One for the gun shop is visible down the road. There is also a stock-car-star billboard on the far right near the safe room.
- **Dead end:** The road toward the mall leads to a **bridge or overpass that is barricaded**: police barriers, a sheriff's car and a tall barrier, with a mix of different blockade types. The survivors say the bridge is blocked and that they should try the door.
- **Damaged car:** A heavily damaged car stands in this area. Tanks often spawn around here (?).
- **Path:** Walk down the street on the right to a **door near the police car**.

### 2.4 First building: a stairway down to the dark storage room
- **Small entry room:** A couple of infected wait inside.
- **Staircase down:** It leads into a **dark storage or basement room**.
  - Crates hold Tier 1 weapons.
  - There is a **rescue closet** (possible grenade launcher or chainsaw).
  - A small back closet holds throwables.
  - More supplies are in the corners and under the stairs.
  - Hordes can come both from the far door and back down the stairs.
- **Exit:** A door leads out to a **lower street level**.

### 2.5 Under the freeway
1. **Turn left** (nothing useful to the right) and go **under the elevated freeway or overpass**. It is a shaded "tunnel" with concrete pillars.
   - A **pickup truck** parked underneath always has a Tier 1 gun, and sometimes a melee weapon and a grenade.
   - Infected run in from both ends.
2. **Looking back:** A **roller-coaster billboard** (the next campaign's theme park) is visible on the overpass.

### 2.6 Open lot past the overpass and the dumpster or container climb
- **The area:** Leaving the tunnel to the left of a parked car, you reach an open area with:
  - a **grassy knoll or hill** on the right (the more direct line);
  - a **tree straight ahead** (grenade launcher or chainsaw spot);
  - a **truck to the right of the tree** with weapons, and a **van** behind it;
  - **portable toilets**;
  - a red **gas can between two bushes**. The gap between the bushes is a shortcut that is good for an ambush.
  - A **moving truck** (rental-van style) offers more weapons.
- **The obstacle:** A concrete wall or barrier closes the area off. A **dumpster or shipping container with a ladder attached** stands against it. Climb the ladder, cross the top, and drop down into the next area.
  - Guides call it both a dumpster and a container. It is a tall metal box with a ladder.
  - Dropping down is effectively one-way in practice. Keep the team together before dropping.
- **Bosses:** A Witch or Tank commonly appears around here or at the next building.

### 2.7 Second building: an office or warehouse with a balcony
1. **After dropping:** Clear the area, and turn **right** for guns or ammo upgrades.
2. **Up to the building:** Stairs lead up to a **concrete platform** (like a loading dock) in front of the building. Check its corners.
3. **Ground floor (dark):** Several rooms, with a closet at the back (grenade launcher or supplies).
4. **Upstairs:** It has:
   - a **health cabinet** (medicine cabinet, often 2 pills);
   - a **melee weapon**;
   - **Tier 2 guns**, the first ones in the campaign, before the gun shop.
   - A Witch is common here.
5. **Balcony:** It overlooks the street. Throw a grenade from here before going down.
   - Hordes climb up from the **porta-potty corner** and over the **fence on the right**.
   - The street area is crossed by dropping or climbing down from here (?).
6. **Possible bus jump:** One source describes a spot where you jump from a broken gate onto a **bus**, with a stock-car-star billboard saying he is at the mall. It is probably around this street section (?).

### 2.8 Sidewalk, upper terrace and the long walkway
1. **Along the sidewalk:** Go **right** along the sidewalk, watching and listening for a Tank. Supplies may be by the portable toilet.
2. **Up to the terrace:** At the **end of the sidewalk, take the stairs up** to a terrace with a **generator**.
   - There are supply spots by the stairs, by the generator, and in the corner on the left.
   - **Bushes at the top hide a side path** where infected wait to flank.
3. **Up to the walkway:** Go around the corner into a **stairwell building** (with a rescue closet) and climb to a **long pedestrian walkway or skybridge** crossing over a road.
   - It has only two entrances, so it is a good place to defend.
   - Hordes come up from the fenced ground below it.
4. **The approach:**
   - A **spotlight** shines on boarded-up shop windows.
   - **Many bodies** lie around the gun shop, shot by the owner.
   - There is a stock-car-star billboard just before the gun shop.
   - A Witch or Tank may appear outside the store.
5. **Down to the shop:** At the far end, **stairs lead down** to the **gun shop entrance**. A survivor shouts that the gun store is just down the stairs.

### 2.9 The gun shop and the owner (scripted)
- **Shop interior:** A single showroom, about 12 × 15 m (est.).
  - **Walls of guns** on racks and pegboards hold every primary weapon in the game except the grenade launcher and M60: Tier 1 SMGs and shotguns, AK, M16, combat rifle, two auto shotguns, hunting rifle, military sniper rifle and a "desert"-named Magnum.
  - Glass display counters, a **laser sight** (always present) and ammo.
  - **First-aid kits** to restock.
  - A fire axe is mentioned as a fixed find here.
  - Handwritten price tags with one-line "tips" for each weapon.
  - Signs of earlier visitors: empty jugs, opened food tins and used paper targets.
- **The owner:** He greets the survivors over an **intercom** as soon as they enter, tells them to help themselves, and asks them to come upstairs.
  - He is an elderly, folksy Southern man, about 60, who calls them bloodstained looters but says they have caught him at a good moment.
  - Interacting with the **intercom button** by the back door makes him buzz open the **electric back door**.
- **Behind the door:**
  - A room to the **right** has supplies. A closet beside the stairs holds extra weapons from a later update.
  - **Stairs lead up to the roof.**
- **Roof:** The owner's **barricaded bunker** is a plywood shack wrapped in razor wire and flying a US flag. Its reinforced door has a **deposit slot or tray**.
  - It deliberately does **not** look like a standard safe-room door, so players don't mistake it for one.
  - He makes his offer through the door:
    - he holed up with plenty of supplies but forgot cola;
    - if they fetch cola from the grocery store "over yonder", he will clear the road to the mall;
    - they can also keep the guns.
  - Coach approves of "a man and his snacks", and Nick is sceptical.
- **The tanker:** Visible from the roof or lot is the **gas tanker truck blocking the road** that leads to the mall.

### 2.10 Save 4 Less grocery crescendo (gauntlet)
- **Geography:**
  - The gun shop and the grocery store are in the **same strip mall** around a **parking lot**.
  - Ground level is split into an **upper lot**, on the gun-shop and stairs side, and a **lower lot** in front of the grocery store. **Hedges and a fence** run between them.
  - The walking route goes the long way **around the hedge and wall on the left**.
  - A **white van** parked by the hedge can be climbed to hop the fence, and **shopping carts** can be stacked against the hedges as steps.
  - There is also a **drop from the stairs straight into the lot** opposite the store. You can't come back up that way, but the cola can be thrown over.
  - Grills, ice and propane tanks are displayed outside the store.
  - A **Witch** can spawn in this lot even though it is a crescendo area. The alarm does not startle her, but kill her first anyway.
- **The store:** A small supermarket.
  - Its windows are **boarded** and its **front doors are alarmed and highlighted**.
  - Inside are **8 aisles** (the numbering skips aisle 4), section signs for frozen foods, produce, bakery and beverages, and loud sale signs.
  - Near the entrance: **2 checkout counters**, 2 vending machines, an ATM, a photocopier and shopping carts.
  - The shelves are **empty**: everything is looted except a few bags of dog food and the **one cola six-pack**.
  - **Fixed contents:** an ammo pile and a fire axe, plus random healing, grenades and a gas can.
- **Trigger:** Opening the front doors sets off a **continuous burglar alarm**, and hordes stream in without stopping.
- **The cola:**
  - It glows at the **back of the store** in the beverage area, priced as a sale item.
  - Whoever carries it **cannot use weapons** and can only shove with the pack.
  - The pack is indestructible and can be dropped or thrown, including over the hedge to a teammate.
- **Delivery:**
  1. Leave the store, go **left around** the hedge and wall, and climb the **stairs back up to the roof**.
  2. Put the cola in the **slot in the bunker door**. This takes a short "use" action.
  3. The owner thanks them. In one joke line he pretends he won't help, then says he was kidding.
  4. He **fires a rocket or grenade** from his rooftop window at the **tanker**, which **explodes**. Anything standing next to it is killed.
- **Ending the alarm:** The blast knocks down a utility pole, which cuts power and **silences the alarm**. Hordes taper off over 1–2 minutes. If the cola is never delivered, the alarm only stops by itself after a very long time (10–20 min).
- **Survivor reaction:** They cheer that the path is clear.

### 2.11 Route to the mall and the safe room
1. **Jump over the rooftop railing** by the bunker and go through the **burning tanker wreck and debris** in the road.
2. The mall is past the tanker. Take the **left entrance** or first left turn toward it.
3. **Mall parking lot with a CEDA staging area:**
   - a "Liberty Mall" sign;
   - CEDA **tents** with weapons and supplies;
   - **desks or folding tables** with ammo upgrades;
   - fences;
   - parked cars, including **alarmed cars** that flash their lights. Shooting or jumping on one sets off its alarm and calls a horde.
   - A laser sight can also spawn in one of the CEDA tents near the safe room.
4. **Bosses:** A Tank or Witch often appears near the end of this chapter if one hasn't already.
5. **Dialogue:** Someone remarks that the mall doesn't look very busy, and someone else guesses everyone must be inside.
6. **Safe room:** At the mall's entrance by the lot. It sits at the back or side entrance of the **department store**.

### 2.12 Item and threat summary for chapter 2
- **Weapons:**
  - Tier 1 in the start safe room, the storage-room crates and the pickup trucks.
  - Tier 2 upstairs in the second building and the full selection in the gun shop.
  - The grenade launcher and chainsaw are commonly found in tents, rescue closets and by the tree.
  - The laser sight is always in the gun shop. Ammo upgrades are on tables and desks.
- **Healing:** First-aid kits in the start safe room, the gun shop and the end safe room. There is a health cabinet in the second building.
- **Gas cans and propane:** By the start trailer and awning, between the bushes, and outside the grocery store.
- **Bosses:** First Witch (second building, the street, or the grocery lot) and first Tank (the upper street, the area past the overpass, near the gun shop or store, or near the end).
- **CEDA infected:** Present and common. This is the best place to collect bile jars.

## 3. Chapter 3: "Mall" (map c1m3_mall)

### At a glance
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Start | Safe room at the back of the department store (ground level of the store) |
| End | Safe room on the mall's **3rd floor**, past a deserted CEDA post |
| Mall structure | 3 public floors (ground, 2nd and 3rd) around open courts with escalators, plus back-of-house service corridors |
| Crescendo | Security alarm (gauntlet). Trigger: **toy-store windows (2nd floor)** or an **alarmed emergency exit door (1st floor)**, chosen by the Director. Turn it off in the **3rd-floor security office** |
| Bosses | A Witch or Tank is possible around the department store, the escalators and the construction area. A Tank in the middle of the crescendo is notorious |
| Hordes | Frequent small mobs (about 10–15 infected every 20–30 s in the original) |
| Typical length | Walking path about 700–900 m with many floor changes (est.) |
| Typical time | 10–15 min (est.). Often considered the hardest map of the campaign |

### 3.1 Opening situation
- **Safe room:**
  - Posters for the stock-car star's appearance (October 14, 4–8 pm).
  - Blood smears on the floor where bodies were dragged.
  - Infected may already be clawing at the barred safe-room door before anyone opens it.
- **Dialogue gist:** Coach asks the group to pray that the food court is all right. Others want to find the evac centre or CEDA, and Rochelle notes that evac centres aren't usually this quiet.
- **Mood:** Most of the mall has **lost power**. Only small lights, emergency lighting and store alarm panels still work, and daylight comes through skylights. The department store is very dark.

### 3.2 Department store (two floors)
1. **Ground floor (dark):**
   - Clothing racks, mannequins (the survivors may laugh if you hit them), clothes thrown on the floor and empty shelves.
   - A **barricade of shelves and boxes** blocks a roll-down door.
   - **From the safe room, go right**, weaving around fixtures toward a **lit corner** where an **escalator** goes up.
   - This is a common Witch or Tank area at the start.
2. **Upper floor:**
   - At the top of the escalator, expect a rush from every side. The far sides of this floor are very dark.
   - Some supplies are **locked inside glass display cases** and have to be smashed out.
   - **Circle around the escalator well** to the store's exit.
3. **Store exit:** A **half-lowered security grille**, propped open by a bench, leads out onto the mall's **2nd-floor concourse**.
   - A Magnum or pistol spawn sits by this gate.
   - A tipped-over **ATM** lies nearby.

### 3.3 Second-floor concourse and the mall directory
- **Directory:** Just outside the department store stands a **mall directory** with a "you are here" marker and the overall plan. It shows the atrium, the alarmed toy store and the survivors' position, but not the direct route, which is blocked by barricades.
- **Concourse:** A large open court with railings overlooking the floor below.
  - Storefronts are a mix of "closing down" and "sale" signs.
  - There are infected that were shot dead against a security gate, and makeshift **blanket shelters**.
  - Move along the **left side** to the dark far corner, then take **either escalator down**. Watch for infected dropping from the floor above.

### 3.4 Ground floor: the food court
1. **Arrival:** At the bottom is a **dark area with a knocked-over planter pot** and often a weapon on the floor.
2. **Food court:** Kiosks, tables and chairs, with fast-food counters around the edge. Invent names in the spirit of a wok place, a taco place, a gyros stand and a barbecue joint.
   - The safest route **keeps to the far-left wall**, through the kiosks and tables, to the **next pair of escalators**.
3. **Restroom hallway:** A long, dark corridor beside these escalators. Sources disagree on whether it is on the left or the right. It leads to toilets and **often has a first-aid kit**, and sometimes a strong weapon.
4. **Joke storefront:** A "Disco Pants and Haircuts" storefront is around here (?).

### 3.5 Back up to the second floor and the one-way drop into the service halls
1. **Up the escalators** to the 2nd floor. Chargers love escalators.
2. At the far end is a **security grille with a slumped corpse**, a supply spot and a weapon.
3. **One-way entry:** A doorway leads to a **ramp or drop over a desk barricade** into the **service hallways**. In practice it is **one-way**: you can't climb back.
   - An **office chair** in a side office can be pushed against the desk to make a step back up.
   - Anyone left behind outside is stuck there. It is a famous ambush spot.

### 3.6 Service corridors (back-of-house)
- **Look:** Narrow, institutional corridors with painted block walls, fluorescent or emergency lighting, offices, storage and toilets.
  - Bathrooms on the right are a good supply spot.
  - Doorways are blocked with **vending machines**, with **floor jacks** left beside them.
  - **Damaged ceiling tiles** suggest infected moving through the ceiling space.
  - A shotgun suicide victim lies here.
  - Hordes tend to come from **behind** you in these halls.
- **Branch (decided by the Director):**
  - **Route A, toy store** (more common; it starts one floor closer to the alarm switch, so it is easier):
    1. Turn left at the corner and follow the corridor to the **back room of the toy store** (2nd floor). Its door is partly open.
    2. The back room has a weapon on the floor, an **ammo pile** and a **health cabinet**.
    3. The **sales floor** has plush toys and other stock and is barricaded. Its **front windows are big plate glass facing the open mall**.
    4. **Shooting or breaking the glass** while inside the store **starts the alarm**. Stray shots or bots can set it off early.
  - **Route B, emergency exit** (longer and harder):
    1. Turn right, go down **two floors**, and pass through **two sets of doors**.
    2. At the bottom is an **emergency exit door** on the ground floor, with a sign warning that an alarm will sound when it is opened.
    3. **Opening it starts the alarm.**
    4. Infected also crawl in through a **vent** near these doors.
    5. In this version the toy store is closed and locked.

### 3.7 The alarm gauntlet
- **The run (from the toy store):**
  1. Leave through the broken windows.
  2. **Ride or run up the escalator directly in front** to the **3rd floor**.
  3. **Run down the 3rd-floor hallway** into the **security office**.
  - From the emergency door you have to climb from the ground floor all the way to the 3rd floor first.
- **Hazards:**
  - Hordes come from everywhere. Smokers pull survivors off escalators, and Chargers and Jockeys push them over planters and railings. Falls to the ground floor are deadly.
  - A **Tank** may be thrown in during the event. If so, fight it on the ground floor rather than up high.
- **Security office (3rd floor):** A room with monitors and an **alarm shut-off switch or panel**. Using it **silences the alarm**.
  - Close the office door afterwards to block the remaining stream.
  - The **rescue closet** for this map is in the security office.
  - Survivors cheer when the alarm stops.

### 3.8 Post-alarm route to the safe room
1. **Down from the office:** A side corridor and **stairs lead from the security office back down to the 2nd floor**.
2. **Back hallway and storage area**, then the **unfinished renovation section**:
   - bare concrete, scaffolding, plastic sheeting, building materials, a **scissor lift**;
   - an optional **large lower area down an escalator**, with a ladder on the scissor lift to climb back up.
3. **Overrun CEDA post:**
   - tents, cots and processing tables;
   - **dead CEDA workers in hazmat suits** (none of them get up; there are no CEDA infected in this chapter);
   - signs of a last stand.
   - Survivors ask aloud what CEDA has been doing.
4. **Updated directory:** A second mall directory shows that the survivors are now close to the car.
5. **Escalator up to the 3rd floor**, then **run to the safe room**. Special Infected can spawn inside it.
   - The escalator direction can differ between runs, facing toward or away from the back wall (?).
   - A Tank or Witch can still appear in the construction area or the last area.

### 3.9 Item and threat summary for chapter 3
- **Notes:** There are no CEDA infected, so bile jars are rare (bring them from earlier chapters). Ammo piles are not guaranteed except in the toy-store back room on route A, so keep spare weapons.
- **Healing:** Restroom first-aid kits (often), the toy-store or route-B health cabinet, and the department-store glass cases.
- **Weapons:** Tier 2 and Tier 1 weapons scattered on floors near gates and corpses. There is a Magnum or pistol by the department-store exit.

## 4. Chapter 4: "Atrium" (finale, map c1m4_atrium)

### At a glance
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Start | 3rd-floor safe room, then a short walk across a bridge to a **glass elevator** |
| Finale starts | Automatically, **when the elevator reaches the ground floor and its doors open** |
| Objective | Collect gas cans and pour them into the stock car on display at the centre of the ground floor |
| Cans needed | **13** in normal co-op on every difficulty. **8** in offline single-player (see notes) |
| Can layout | 1 fixed can in front of the elevator, plus **6 pairs** at random from **8 possible spots**: 1 on the ground floor, 4 on the 2nd floor and 3 on the 3rd floor |
| Bosses | Tanks during the finale (none if you are fast enough). A sitting Witch is possible near the elevator |
| End | All survivors at the car; cutscene of the car crashing out through the front doors; freeze-frame; credits |
| Typical time | 7–12 min (est.) |

**Can count notes:**
- The community wiki says 8 cans are needed in single-player (offline) and 13 in every other mode. It adds that hosting an online lobby alone still asks for 13, even on Easy.
- A StrategyWiki page instead says "8 on Normal, 13 on Expert". This conflicts with the other source and is likely wrong (?).
- For the remake, the simplest faithful rule is **13 cans**, with an optional "solo" setting of 8.

### 4.1 Opening: safe room to elevator
- **Safe room contents:**
  - posters of the race driver;
  - early graffiti showing people realising that CEDA isn't coming;
  - a small graffiti tribute on the left of the door.
- **Dialogue gist:**
  - Nick asks who the race-car guy on all the posters is.
  - Ellis and Coach explain that he is a local stock-car legend, "the pride of Georgia". Ellis gushes, and Nick says the man looks like a jerk.
  - Ellis tells Nick that he can joke about Savannah but must not insult the driver.
  - On the way out someone says that if they meet a zombified driver, someone else will have to kill him.
- **The path:**
  1. A short corridor with **restrooms** (worth looting: medkits, upgrades, pills, adrenaline).
  2. A mall map **without** a "you are here" dot.
  3. A corner with **construction framing**: a plank, a gap and a support frame.
  4. A **3rd-floor bridge across the atrium** to the **glass elevator**.
- **No infected on the ground floor before the elevator ride.** A **sitting Witch** is possible along this path or right at the elevator.

### 4.2 Elevator ride
- **Trigger:** All survivors get in and press the button.
- **The ride:** The glass car descends about 3 floors in roughly 20–30 s (est.), giving a full view of the atrium. Gas cans glow with **white outlines** and the car is visible in the middle.
- **Dialogue gist:**
  - Coach asks if anyone has an idea, since there will be no evacuation.
  - Ellis suggests taking the driver's stock car and fuelling it, because cars at shows are kept with empty tanks.
  - Coach agrees, sometimes apologising to the driver, and says to run for gas as soon as the doors open.

### 4.3 Atrium layout (for blockout)
The atrium is a large central space, often described as roughly **circular or oval**, open through **three public floors**. Above them is a **4th-level ledge** that only infected can reach, and a **skylight or glass roof** lets in strong daylight.

Proposed proportions: footprint about **60–80 m × 40–50 m**, floor-to-floor about **5–6 m**, total height to the skylight about **20 m** (est.).

- **Ground floor (main court):**
  - **Display stage or platform** in the middle, holding the **stock car**, which faces the **main entrance doors**.
    - The car is a late-1960s winged stock car (Charger Daytona style), with a tall rear wing and a mostly blue paint scheme (?).
    - It is surrounded by promotional banners and display props for the racer.
    - Tanks **cannot punch the car**. You can drop onto its roof from the 2nd floor without taking fall damage.
  - **Central information kiosk:** a service counter with **4 first-aid kits and an ammo pile** in plain view on its counters.
    - Its roof can be climbed from a nearby **planter pot**, near a dead end with a hole in the wall.
  - **Planters, benches and storefronts** around the edge, with some **doors where hordes burst out**. Closets and service doors serve as spawn points.
  - The **glass elevator shaft** on one side. The **single fixed gas can** sits right in front of its doors.
  - The **main entrance:** a large set of glass front doors.
    - In the cutscene the car smashes through them.
    - Outside there is **scaffolding** and pallets of **masonry mortar bags**, because the mall was being renovated.
- **Staircases:**
  - **A staircase very close to the car** climbs straight to the 3rd floor. Guides describe it as a spiral or switchback (?). This is the fastest way up.
  - A **staircase on the elevator's side** leads to the 2nd floor.
  - "Infected-only" ladders let Special Infected climb between floors (4 on the top floor in the survival variant).
- **2nd and 3rd floors:**
  - **Ring walkways** with **glass railings**, which Chargers can break, and store frontages.
  - Some walkway sections have **glass floor panels** that can be **shot out to drop down one floor**, a key shortcut.
  - On the 3rd floor, one spot between the stairs and a pillar has **no railing**.
  - **Large windows** along the back.
  - A **3rd-floor bridge** spans the atrium. It leads to the back-left corner, where a pair of cans often sits next to a door infected come through.
  - In a 2nd-floor back corner there is **construction scaffolding** that makes a safe drop to the ground floor.
- **4th level:** A ledge right above the car, usable by Special Infected as perches.

### 4.4 Finale mechanics
- **Start:** As soon as the elevator doors open, the finale begins. The on-screen objective says to find gas cans, though the original oddly shows a generic "rescue is coming" message.
- **Can behaviour:**
  - Cans glow white, turn yellow while carried, and show an orange outline once dropped.
  - Carrying a can takes the place of your weapon. You can throw it, including down from upper floors toward the car.
  - Pouring is a **hold-use action of about 2.5 s at the car's filler**. Taking damage restarts it, and adrenaline speeds it up.
  - A destroyed can respawns where it was first found. In competitive modes, Spitter acid ignites dropped cans.
  - Cans carried over from earlier chapters count toward the total.
- **Progress display:** A **HUD counter at the top of the screen** (cans poured out of 13), driven by a scavenge-progress entity. No in-world scoreboard is confirmed in the sources (?). A large in-world sign or banner could stand in for it in the remake.
- **Supplies near can spots:** throwables, pills or adrenaline, melee weapons, and a **defibrillator**, often on the 3rd floor near the big windows.
- **Waves and pacing:**
  - The Director sends **mini-hordes** that tend to be set off as survivors enter hotspots around the atrium. They are smaller than most crescendo hordes and have to run a long way.
  - **Special Infected** appear from time to time.
  - **Tanks** arrive periodically after a lull in common infected.
  - In practice the cycle is roughly hordes → lull → Tank → lull → hordes (?). The exact stage script isn't confirmed.
  - The **first Tank arrives late enough** that a fast team can finish before it shows up (PC achievement: "escape before the first Tank").
  - If the team takes too long, the Director escalates to **near-continuous hordes and repeated Tanks**.
  - While a Tank is alive, fewer common infected spawn, which leaves a window for pouring.
- **Common strategy:**
  1. Run up the stairs by the car to the 3rd floor.
  2. Clear the cans there and throw them down near the car.
  3. Shoot out the glass floor to drop to the 2nd floor and repeat.
  4. Drop to the ground via the scaffolding or the kiosk roof.
  5. One player with adrenaline pours while the others feed cans to them.

### 4.5 Ending
- **Fuel full:** Once the last can is poured, the survivors shout that the car is ready and everyone should get in. Hordes and Tanks keep coming.
- **Escape trigger:** All living survivors must reach the car or its display platform. Anyone still incapacitated is left behind and counted as dead.
- **Outro cutscene:**
  - Survivors in the car shout things like "punch it, Ellis" or apologise to the driver.
  - The car **smashes out through the mall's front glass doors** into a crowd of infected and **launches into a jump**.
  - The shot **freezes mid-air**, in the style of an old TV car show.
  - Credits and stats follow.
- **Story hook:** The survivors drive toward New Orleans. The next campaign continues with this car.

### 4.6 Item and threat summary for chapter 4
- **Fixed:** 4 first-aid kits and an ammo pile on the kiosk.
- **Random:** Restroom supplies on the approach, plus throwables, pills, adrenaline, a defibrillator, melee weapons and the occasional Tier 2 weapon by the can spots.
- **For reference:** The competitive "Scavenge" version of this map uses 16 fixed can spots (3 on the ground floor, 7 on the 2nd, 6 on the 3rd), and its start area has 3 adrenaline shots on a kiosk. This is a useful list of plausible can positions for the remake.
- **Threats:**
  - A sitting Witch near the elevator (rare).
  - Tanks during the finale. A Tank can knock survivors off the walkways; a fall from the 3rd floor kills.
  - A Spitter or Hunter perched above the car while survivors pour.
  - A very rare zombified driver in a fireproof racing suit with very high health.

## 8. Open Questions and Low-Confidence Details (?)
- **Building height:** The hotel's exact storey count (sources say both 6 and 8). The rooms visited suggest floors 8 and 7.
- **Roof dressing:** Whether there are CEDA tents or a large hotel-name sign on the hotel roof.
- **Chapter 2 order:** The exact order of the bus or broken-gate jump in chapter 2, and whether the second building's balcony is dropped from or climbed down.
- **Whitaker's doors:** How many electric doors the owner opens. One source mentions a "first" electric door, suggesting there may be a second at the top of the stairs.
- **Mall restrooms:** Which side the food-court restroom hallway is on (sources say both left and right).
- **Construction-area escalator:** Whether its direction or position really varies between runs.
- **Finale script:** The exact wave and Tank sequence, and whether there is any physical scoreboard in the atrium. Only the HUD counter is confirmed.
- **The car:** Its paint colour, livery and number. The wiki only compares it to famous blue winged cars of 1969–70.
- **Can count:** The single-player count is 8 per the wiki; one guide's "difficulty-based" claim is doubtful.
- **Horde music:** Campaign-specific horde music style.
