PACKAGE: nature   (order: 28; build() places nothing except optional systems; layouts call src/content/nature/api.js)

Build nature and winter-effects:
- Trees (bare winter trees with angular branches emerging from snow): birch (white bark with black marks, drooping thin branch tips — use the non-solid 'twig' material for fine twigs so the player isn't blocked, solid trunk), poplar (tall columnar, very common in Moscow courtyards, often pollarded/cut back), maple, linden, apple (small, gnarled), spruce/fir (dark, snow-laden layers), plus young saplings with a support stake, lilac/currant bushes, dry weeds and reed stalks poking out of snow. Snow on branches (sparse thin lines of snow on top of horizontal branches). Generate ~5–8 seed variants per species procedurally (recursive branching rasterized into voxels) and keep them instancing-friendly (a limited set of variant models reused with random rotation — not a unique model per tree). Use LODs. Trees must look good both against a dark sky lit from below by sodium lamps and against a white day sky.
- placeTree(ctx, {x, z, species, size, variant, rot}) and scatterTrees(ctx, {rect:[x0,z0,x1,z1] | polygon, count | density, species: {birch: 0.6, poplar: 0.4}, avoid(x,z) => bool, minSpacing, seed}).
- placePond(ctx, {x, z, rx, rz, seed}) — frozen pond: lower the terrain bowl slightly, ice material with snow patches and cracks, reeds at the shore, a snow-covered shore path, maybe a sign «Выход на лёд запрещён!» (Walking on the ice is prohibited!) via ctx.signs.
- snow helpers for layouts: snowBank(ctx, x0,z0,x1,z1, height) along roads (lumpy, dirty on the road side), snowDrift(ctx, x, z, ...) against walls/fences, plowedPile(ctx, ...) big dirty piles at the end of driveways (with a buried thing sticking out sometimes).
- Steam: addSteam(ctx, {x, y, z, rate, size}) — a cheap GPU particle plume (soft billboards, lit by fog/scatter colours via the shared uniforms; reuse src/render/shaderChunks.js fog functions) for manholes, heat pipes, vents of the boiler house, car exhausts. One system for all plumes (single draw call), updated in update().
- Crows: addCrows(ctx, {x, y, z, count, perch:'tree'|'wire'|'roof'}) — small dark voxel crows perched that occasionally caw ('crow_caw') and fly off ('crows_takeoff') when the player comes within ~12 m, flapping away (2–3 frame flap via swapping visible models of dynamic placements) and disappearing into the fog; they come back later. Keep counts small.
- Falling snow clumps: occasionally (rarely) a clump of snow falls from a tree branch near the player with a soft 'snow_fall_branch' sound and a puff of particles.
Verify with viewer shots (each species, noon and 21:00 --lamp) and an in-world shot with --only base,nature where your build() (or a temporary test you remove afterwards) places a few trees near the spawn (x≈248, z≈515 is on the avenue sidewalk; use e.g. x 230, z 420 instead).
