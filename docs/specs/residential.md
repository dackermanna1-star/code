PACKAGE: residential   (order: 25 for build helpers; your index.js build() itself places nothing — layout packages call your API)

Build the residential building library — the most important visual element of the game (the reference photos are 5-, 9- and 16/17-storey Soviet panel blocks at night with grids of warm and cold windows).

SERIES (choose dimensions from docs/research/architecture.md; floor-to-floor 11 voxels):
- 'p44' — P-44 9-storey and 17-storey panel sections (white/grey panels, blue-grey or terracotta tile inserts, loggias).
- 'ii49' — II-49 9-storey (beige/white tiled panel look, characteristic window grid, balconies).
- '1515' — 1-515/9Sh 9-storey (grey panels, coloured accent stripes, ribbed loggia panels).
- 'p44t' — P-44T 17-storey with bay windows (эркеры) — the "newer" 1997+ look.
- 'k7' — 5-storey khrushchyovka (panel K-7 look or brick II-32 variant), no elevator, smaller windows, balconies.
Each series = section models (one podyezd each; build 2–3 seed variants per section with different balcony glazing / curtains-frames / AC-free (2008: few AC units, some satellite dishes, laundry lines on balconies), plus end-cap variants if needed (blind gable walls with panel seams). Sections are placed side-by-side along local +X to form long buildings; corner/turn handled by the caller placing two buildings.

EACH SECTION MUST HAVE: plinth (цоколь) with basement vents (продухи), raised ground floor, entrance on the front side (local −Z) with porch steps, canopy (козырёк) with snow on it, metal entrance door opening (door itself is a separate dynamic model 'res_door_entrance' with hinge pivot), intercom panel, plate «Подъезд N / кв. X–Y» (sign), staggered stairwell windows above the entrance (half-landings) glazed with glass_clear and a simplified stairwell volume behind them in EVERY section (walls, landings, flights, a bulb voxel emissive lamp_bulb on some landings so stairwells read as lit from outside; use m.addInterior for the stairwell volume), apartment windows with frames (window material with a unique nextWindowId per window — required for the procedural lit-window shader), loggias/balconies with varied glazing (wood frames, some open with snow and junk, some glazed with white plastic), roof parapet with snow, elevator machine-room box (9+ storeys), vent stacks, TV antennas (thin), a satellite dish here and there, rust streaks from balconies, panel seams.

ENTERABLE SECTIONS (opt-in per section): a fully explorable stairwell from the entrance tambour up to the top floor: tambour with inner door, mailboxes row (blue/grey), steps up to the 1st floor landing, elevator door(s) with call button, landings with 4 apartment doors (padded leatherette with buttons, metal doors, numbers), garbage chute hatch, radiators, electrical cabinet, two-tone paint (dark green/blue lower, whitewash upper), tile floors, graffiti/scratched texts, windows on half-landings looking out over the courtyard, weak bulbs (lights domain:'indoor', some flickering 'buzz', some dead — "one working light among several broken ones"), zones (reverb 'stairwell', indoor:true, snow:false). Elevator: working cab (dynamic placement) that you can call and ride between floors (interaction buttons, doors, sounds 'elevator_call' 'elevator_doors' 'elevator_motor' 'elevator_stop'; inside the cab: burned buttons, wood-pattern laminate). Apartment doors: interaction "Позвонить" (ring) → 'doorbell' sound, nobody answers (very rarely: shuffling steps 'footsteps_phantom' then silence). Muffled 'tv_muffled'/'voices_muffled' emitters behind a few doors (create lazily when ctx.audio.ready). Entrance door: interaction to open (enterable) — opens, auto-closes after a few seconds with 'door_metal_slam' + 'door_magnet_lock'; non-enterable doors: "Заперто / домофон" → 'intercom_beep' then 'intercom_call' ringing with no answer. 
(Dropped by the lead: the `loopStairs` anomaly option is NOT needed — do not spend effort on it; if it already exists, leave it unused and undocumented as optional.)

API (src/content/residential/api.js):
  placeResidential(ctx, { series, floors (5|9|12|16|17 per series support), sections, x, z, rot, seed, enterable: [sectionIndexes], address: { street: 'ул. Берёзовая', house: '12', korpus: '2' }, firstEntrance: 1, firstApartment: 1, tint?: accent colour hex })
  → { placements, bounds: {minX,minZ,maxX,maxZ}, entrances: [{ index, number, x, y, z, angle (front facing), doorPlacement, enterable }], height }
  The (x, z) is the building's front-left corner or centre — document precisely. Also add house-number plates on the building corners (blue plate «ул. Берёзовая, 12 к.2»), entrance lamps (lights.add with schedule 'night', ~20% broken or flickering), navPaths from each entrance a few meters outward, registry.entrances push, tags 'building' / 'entrance'.
Also export the raw model names in the catalog with dimensions so layout agents can plan footprints (e.g. a 9-storey P-44 building of 4 sections is ~X m long, Y m deep, Z m tall).

Make the windows look right at night (unique window ids, recessed 1 voxel, frames), the stairwells read as lit staggered windows from outside, and the facades feel weathered and repetitive in the good, liminal way. Verify with viewer shots at noon and at 21:00 (--lamp), and an in-world shot of a placed building with an enterable stairwell (walk inside with --at at the stairwell coordinates and shoot).
