# Attachment, ammunition and skin reference

Generated by `tools/datagen/generate_docs.py` from the shipped data. Modifiers are applied as `(base + Σadd) × Πmultiply`, then clamped to each stat's limits; the gunsmith screen shows the exact before/after values for the weapon being modified.

**Field changes:** optics, muzzle devices, grips, magazines, tactical devices and charms can be swapped anywhere (default `fieldCustomization: "quick_detach"`); barrels and stocks need a Gunsmith Bench within 8 blocks.

## Skins

Every weapon accepts every skin (10 finishes × 12 weapons = 120 variants, plus the standard finish). Skins are cosmetic only. Finishes marked *kit* consume a Prismatic Finish Kit when applied in survival (configurable with `rareSkinsRequireKit`); animated finishes glow and pulse.
