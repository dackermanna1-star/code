**Title:** What happens if you're stranded in the middle of the ocean?

**Description:**

What really happens when you're alone in the open ocean, with no boat, no land and no life jacket? From cold shock and exhaustion to sunburn, thirst and the night, this is how the body copes, why seawater makes dehydration worse, and what actually decides whether you're found in time.

Survival depends heavily on water temperature, whether you can stay afloat, and whether anyone knows you're missing. Times vary enormously with conditions, so this video avoids giving exact survival times.

If you ever end up in the water: float to live. Tilt your head back with your ears under, relax and try to breathe normally, and gently move your hands and legs to stay afloat (RNLI).
