# LIMINAL MOSCOW: Residential architecture of a Moscow outskirts microdistrict, winter 2008

Confidence key: **[W]** means checked against Russian Wikipedia or mos.ru. **≈** means my estimate from typical practice; use it as a plausible game value, not survey data.

## 0. Global conventions (voxel = 0.25 m)

- **Floor-to-floor height:** 2.75–2.85 m in almost every series (2.64 m ceiling plus a 0.14 m slab). Use **11 voxels (2.75 m)** for all panel series. For a 5-storey khrushchyovka or II-18 you can also use 11 voxels; 10 voxels is a little low.
- **Wall thickness:** exterior panels are 0.30–0.40 m thick, so use 1–2 voxels. The visible window recess is about 0.15–0.25 m, which is 1 voxel.
- **Overall heights:**

| Storeys | Height incl. plinth, technical floor and parapet |
|---|---|
| 5 | ≈ 15–16 m |
| 9 | ≈ 28–30 m |
| 12 | ≈ 37 m |
| 16–17 | ≈ 47–51 m (P-44 ≈ 47 m [W]) |
| 22 | ≈ 65 m |

- **Plinth (цоколь):** 0.8–1.4 m high, painted dark grey-brown `#5B5048` or faced with tile. It has square vents (продухи) about 0.25 × 0.25 m every few metres. A cat often sits in one, and there is sometimes a handwritten note: «Не закрывайте продухи — там живут кошки» ("Don't block the vents, cats live here").
- **Standard Soviet wooden window units:**

| Unit | Size (H × W) | Voxels |
|---|---|---|
| ОР 15-15 | ≈ 1.5 × 1.5 m | 6 × 6 |
| ОР 15-18 (living room) | 1.5 × 1.8 m | 6 × 7 |
| ОР 15-12 (kitchen) | 1.5 × 1.2 m | 6 × 5 |
| Balcony door | ≈ 2.2 × 0.75 m | 9 × 3, next to a 1.2 m window |

  The sill sits 0.8 m above the floor (3 voxels). Windows repeat about every 3.0–3.6 m, one per room.
- **Frames in 2008:** about 50–60% are still old double wooden frames painted white (paint flaking, grey `#D8D4C8`). The rest are white PVC replacements, with a few brown "wood-look" PVC (`#5A3A26`).
- **Panel seams:** a horizontal seam at every floor line and vertical seams at every room bay (2.7–3.6 m). Seams are 20–30 mm wide with mastic sealant `#3C3B38`. Patchy re-sealed seams show as darker vertical streaks, and there are rusty run-off stains under balconies (`#8A5A3A` at 30% opacity).

### Facade hex palette
- **P-44 tile:** "signal white" `#ECEBE6`; blue `#3D6DAE` (a 1984 P-44 is recorded with "signal white" and "blue" tile [mos.ru]); brown `#7A4B32`; beige `#D2BC97`. Coloured tile forms horizontal bands under windows and solid fields on the end walls.
- **P-44T:** terracotta `#B4533C`, orange `#D9823B`, yellow `#E2C25B`, cream bands `#EDE6D6`.
- **II-49 small tile** (≈ 48 × 48 mm or 21 × 21 mm "carpet tile"): off-white `#DCDAD1` mixed with pale blue `#9DB7CF`, pale yellow `#E3D7A2` or salmon `#D9A28C` in checker or stripe "carpets". **Fallen-tile scars** are raw concrete `#8E8C86` patches about 0.3–1 m across, typically 3–8 per facade.
- **Weathered concrete / 1-515:** `#A9A79F`; wet-streaked `#7F7D77`.
- **Grime:** a darker band 0–2 m above ground, splashed with slush (`#6E6A60`).

## 2. Balconies and loggias

- **Balconies:** the slab projects 0.8–1.2 m and is 2.4–3.6 m wide (10–14 voxels). The railing or parapet is 1.0–1.1 m high. Fronts are either concrete panels (often ribbed) or steel bar railings `#3A3A36`.
- **Loggias:** recessed 1.2–1.5 m.
- **Glazing in 2008:** about 70–80% are glazed, all in different ways, and creators should randomise per unit:
  - aluminium sliding frames, white `#F2F2EE` or bare silver;
  - old wooden frames painted white, sometimes a single frame missing a pane and patched with plywood or cardboard;
  - new white PVC;
  - sheet cladding below the glass: beige siding `#D9CBA8`, white profiled sheet, blue corrugated sheet `#4E6E9A`, or wood "вагонка" lining;
  - **extensions (выносы)** pushed 20–30 cm past the slab edge;
  - top-floor owners adding pitched corrugated **mini-roofs** with icicles hanging off them.
- **Unglazed balconies:** laundry frozen stiff on lines, skis, a sled, three-litre jars, a car tyre, an old armchair, a bicycle under snow.
- **Other facade clutter:** satellite dishes (parody the NTV+ satellite service as «ТВ-Плюс»), maybe 1–3 per section. Split air-conditioner units, rarer in a 2008 outskirts block (1–5 per facade). **Window grilles on floor 1 and often floor 2**: welded steel bars with a "sunburst" (солнышко) pattern, painted white or black.

## 3. Roofs

- Flat bitumen roof with internal drains [W: KOPE, P-3]. Parapet 0.6–1.2 m, with galvanised flashing on top `#9AA0A3`.
- **Lift machine rooms:** a brick or concrete box above each entrance, about 2.5–3 m above roof level and 3 × 5 m in plan, with a louvred vent and a steel door. In 9-storey blocks it sits on top of the technical attic.
- **Exit hatch or door:** «Выход на кровлю закрыт. Ключи в ДЕЗ» ("Roof exit locked. Keys at the DEZ", the district housing directorate). A padlock.
- **Antennas:** collective TV antennas (Yagi or "wave channel" rods on 3–4 m masts with guy wires) and rusty abandoned dipoles. Cable-TV and ISP cables are draped over the parapet.
- **Winter:** snow cornices 0.3–0.5 m deep overhang the parapet, and icicles up to 1 m hang at the drip edges. Signs read «Осторожно! Сход снега и наледи» ("Caution: falling snow and ice"). Red-and-white tape is stretched between posts below the facade.

## 4. Entrance (подъезд)

- **Porch:** a concrete platform 1.5–2.5 m deep and 2.5–3.5 m wide, with 1–3 steps outside at 0.15 m risers.
  - In P-44 and II-49 there are another 6–9 steps up inside to the first-floor or lift level. The ground floor sits about 1.0–1.2 m above grade.
  - **Stroller ramp:** two steel channel rails about 0.12 m wide and 0.5 m apart, welded along the steps.
  - Steel pipe handrail painted black or green.
  - A sand or rock-salt box (wooden, 0.8 × 0.5 × 0.5 m) with a shovel.
- **Canopy (козырёк):** a concrete slab 0.12–0.15 m thick, ≈ 1.5–2 m deep, ≈ 3–3.5 m wide, underside at ≈ 2.4–2.6 m.
  - The top is roofing felt, and in 2008 is often re-roofed in corrugated sheet or polycarbonate.
  - On top: snow, cigarette butts, a beer bottle, a lost mitten.
  - One lamp above the door: an incandescent bulb in a round frosted "tablet" fixture (НПП, ≈ 0.25 m diameter) or a bulb in a wire cage. Warm `#FFD7A0`, often flickering.
- **Door:** steel, 1.0–1.2 m × 2.1 m, painted hammer-finish brown `#5A3E2E`, grey `#6F7270` or dark green `#2F4A3A`.
  - A hydraulic closer on top (often broken, so the door slams).
  - An **electromagnetic lock** on the head jamb.
  - A small window with grid glass, or none at all.
- **Intercom panel (домофон):** at ≈ 1.4–1.5 m height beside the door.
  - Brushed steel or aluminium, about 0.12 × 0.30 m.
  - A 3–4-digit red 7-segment display `#FF2A1A`.
  - A 3 × 4 keypad, 0–9 plus **«В»** (вызов, call) and **«С»** (сброс, reset). Key numbers are worn blank.
  - A round **"pill" (таблетка) contact-key reader** (iButton-style).
  - A speaker grille.
  - Real reference makers: Визит, Цифрал, Элтис, Метаком. Possible parodies: «ВИЗАТ», «ЦИФРОН», «ЭЛЬТАС».
  - 2008 prices: copying a key cost 100–200 руб.; the maintenance fee was 25–40 руб./month per flat.
- **Plate above the door:** blue `#1F4E96` with white text, about 0.4 × 0.2 m, for example «Подъезд 2 · кв. 37–72» ("Entrance 2 · flats 37–72"). Keep the flat ranges consistent with the series:

| Series | Flats per entrance | Example ranges |
|---|---|---|
| 9 storeys × 4 flats | 36 | 1–36, 37–72, 73–108 |
| 17 storeys × 4 flats | 68 | 1–68, 69–136 |
| K-7, 5 × 3 flats | 15 | 1–15, 16–30 |

- **Notice board:** an «Информация» board under plexiglass, plus papers taped to the door:
  - «Уважаемые жители! Закрывайте дверь подъезда!» ("Dear residents! Close the entrance door!")
  - «Будьте бдительны! О подозрительных предметах сообщайте по тел. 02» ("Be vigilant! Report suspicious objects to 02"). This is an anti-terror poster standard since 1999.
  - «ОДС тел. …» (the district dispatcher's phone number)
  - «Куплю 1–2-комн. квартиру в вашем доме. Без посредников» ("Will buy a 1–2-room flat in your building. No agents")
  - «Интернет 8 Мбит/с — 600 руб./мес.» ("Internet 8 Mbit/s for 600 rub./month")
  - «Пропал кот» ("Lost cat"), with tear-off phone strips
  - Emergency numbers in 2008: 01 fire, 02 **милиция** (police; the name was still "militsiya" then), 03 ambulance, 04 gas.
  - Phone numbers in the format 8-495-xxx-xx-xx or 8-916/903/926-xxx-xx-xx.
  - The management body in 2008 is the **ДЕЗ / ГУ ИС района …**. The «Жилищник» organisations did not exist yet.

## 5. House-number plates

- **Pre-2013 Moscow standard:** white letters on blue. The colour is described as a historical Moscow tradition [mos.ru], and the current unified navigation system only began in 2014 [mos.ru].
- **Typical 2008 version:** a corner plate or illuminated light box, about 0.9 × 0.5 m.
  - Street on top in a condensed sans-serif: «ул. Академика Ветрова».
  - Big number below: «14», with «корп. 2» or «к. 2» smaller.
  - Many boxes are dead or cracked, and old enamel ones (white with black letters) survive on khrushchyovkas.
- **Address formats:** «д. 14, корп. 2» (building 14, block 2), «стр. 1» (structure, for non-residential), «вл. 5» (land holding). A microdistrict address often reads «Тополиный б-р, д. 7, корп. 3».
- **Painted end-wall numbers:** large digits (1–2 m tall) painted near the top of a blank end wall, e.g. «14/2» or «КОРП. 2». This is typical of microdistrict planning.

## 6. Stairwell interiors

- **Paint:** gloss oil paint on the lower 1.5–1.7 m, then a 1–2 cm dark dividing stripe `#2B2B28`, then **whitewash** above and on the ceiling (`#E6E2D3`, dirtied to `#C9C5B8`). Lower colours:

| Colour | Hex |
|---|---|
| Dark green | `#3F6B4F` |
| Olive | `#6B7A4A` |
| Blue | `#3E5F8A` |
| Pale blue | `#8FB0CC` |
| Brown | `#6B3F2A` |
| Peach-beige (2000s repaint) | `#D8B48C` |

  The ceiling carries **black soot spots from burnt matches** that kids flicked up to stick.
- **Floors:** metlakh tile, 150 × 150 mm or 100 × 100 mm, either brown `#7A4A32` and beige `#C9B28E` checkerboard or plain red-brown, with chipped and missing tiles. Flights are precast terrazzo-concrete (`#8C8A84` with flecks).
- **Stair geometry:** two flights per floor of 9 risers each (≈ 0.155 × 0.30 m), flight width ≈ 1.05–1.2 m. Steel balusters painted dark, with a brown PVC handrail `#5A3A28` (often missing or melted).
- **Half-landing window** (9-storey series): ≈ 1.2 × 1.2 m, a single-glazed wooden frame painted many times. Some panes are cracked or replaced with plywood. On the sill: a coffee or sprat tin used as an ashtray, and a dead plant.
- **Mailboxes:** on the ground-floor landing, in rows of 4–6 boxes per unit.
  - Old: perforated steel painted green or blue, with stencilled white numbers.
  - 2000s replacements: grey `#9EA3A6` or blue `#3C5A9A` powder-coat.
  - Each box ≈ 0.30 W × 0.35 H × 0.20 D m, stuffed with flyers.
  - A cardboard box on the floor for discarded flyers.
- **Radiators:** cast-iron MS-140 ribbed units, 0.59 m high, 93 mm per section, 8–12 sections, painted the wall colour. Steel pipe risers hiss and clank.
- **Garbage chute:** a ≈ 0.4 m diameter steel or asbestos-cement pipe with a hinged hopper valve (≈ 0.4 × 0.3 m) at 0.8 m height.
  - Location by series: on half-landings in II-49 and II-18 [W]; on every floor in P-3 and KOPE [W].
  - At ground level, a refuse room with a steel door (мусорокамера).
- **Floor electrical cabinet (ЩЭ, floor panel):** ≈ 0.8 W × 1.1 H m in a wall niche, painted over, the door often ajar.
  - Inside: Soviet disc meters plus new digital meters.
  - A yellow triangle with a lightning bolt: «Опасно! Электрическое напряжение» ("Danger! Electrical voltage"). The old warning «Не влезай — убьёт!» ("Keep out, it will kill you!") also turns up.
  - A low-voltage box with an ISP switch, **blinking green LEDs**, and Ethernet cables stapled along the walls.
  - 2008 ISPs, with parody names: Корбина → «Корвина», Акадо → «Аркада», Стрим → «Поток».
- **Lights:** "tablet" ceiling fixtures (НПП 03, ≈ 0.25 m diameter) with 40–60 W incandescent bulbs `#FFD9A0`. About one in four is dead, and some are bare bulbs on a cord. Motion sensors are rare in 2008.
- **Landing layouts:**
  - **9-storey (II-49, 1-515):** 4 flat doors on the landing, the lift in the stair core, the chute on the half-landing.
  - **P-44, P-3 and KOPE:** a lift lobby (2–3 lifts plus the chute), a corridor to 4 flats that residents have closed off with their own **steel lobby door (тамбурная дверь)**, and an exit onto an **open-air transfer balcony** to the **smoke-proof stair (незадымляемая лестница)**. On every floor you step outside into wind and snow.
  - **Basement and attic doors:** «Посторонним вход воспрещён» ("No unauthorised entry"), padlocked.

## 7. Lift (Mogilev, MLZ, or Moscow's own Karacharovo plant)

- **Cab size:**

| Lift | Cab plan (W × D) | Height | Door |
|---|---|---|---|
| 400 kg passenger | ≈ 1.1 × 0.95 m (the size allowed until 1991 under GOST 5746 [dnaop]) | ≈ 2.1 m | 0.7 m centre-opening |
| 630 kg freight-passenger (the "coffin lift") | 1.1 × 2.1 m or 2.1 × 1.1 m [dnaop] | | ≈ 0.9–1.0 m |

- **Interior:** laminated chipboard in a wood-grain pattern (`#A07850` or `#B08960`), aluminium corner trim, brown linoleum floor `#5A4636`.
  - The ceiling has a frosted plastic panel over a buzzing, flickering fluorescent tube, with soot circles.
  - The button panel is a vertical column of round black buttons with white numerals. 2–4 are **melted with a lighter** into brown craters.
  - A bell-icon «Вызов» button, a speaker grille for the dispatcher («Диспетчер слушает», "Dispatcher here"), and sometimes a «Стоп» button.
  - A plate: «Грузоподъёмность 400 кг · 5 человек» ("Capacity 400 kg · 5 people").
  - Scratched into the plastic: «Курить запрещено» ("No smoking"), Spartak and CSKA football slogans, a phone number.
- **Landing side:** grey-painted doors with the floor number stencilled on them. A round call button glows red or orange once pressed. Sign: «Лифт не работает» ("Lift out of order").
- **Sound design (synth):**
  1. The relay **clack** in the machine room, which carries down the shaft.
  2. Doors closing: a motor whine of about 200–400 Hz, then a bang.
  3. Motor hum at 50/100/150 Hz with a rising whine.
  4. A periodic **"tuk"** as the cab passes each landing switch, about every 2.8 s at 1 m/s.
  5. Cable and rail rattle.
  6. Slowdown: the two-speed motor drops to a lower-pitched hum.
  7. The brake clunks, the doors whirr open, and the light buzzes throughout.
  8. Overload buzzer: about 1 kHz, continuous.

## 8. Apartment doors

- **Soviet wooden door upholstered in dermatin (leatherette)** over batting. Colours: dark brown `#4A2C1F`, burgundy `#5A1E24`, black `#1E1C1B`, olive `#3E4430`. Brass-headed upholstery nails are strung with wire in a diamond grid (≈ 15 cm diamonds). There is a brass peephole and aluminium or brass numerals «47».
- **2008 steel doors** (about 60–70% of doors): brown hammer finish or vinyl-wrapped MDF in "oak" or "walnut", with two locks.
- **Doorbell:** a small white button beside the frame. It plays a bird-trill or a melody chime (≈ 1–2 kHz square-wave tones).

## 9. Graffiti, scratches, stencils

- Marker and scratched texts: «Цой жив» ("Tsoi lives", for the late rock singer Viktor Tsoi), «Спартак — чемпион», «ЦСКА», «КОНИ» (fan nickname for CSKA), «Лена + Саша = любовь», «Здесь был Вася» ("Vasya was here", the classic), «Коля лох» ("Kolya's a loser"), «Даша, я тебя люблю» ("Dasha, I love you"), «Эмо — отстой» ("Emo sucks"; emo was big in 2008), «Хой!» (the punk shout of the band Sektor Gaza), «КиШ» (the band Korol i Shut), «Rammstein», «Linkin Park», an anarchy Ⓐ, and dated tags «2007».
- **Pinned to walls and doors:**
  - Flyer stickers: «Вскрытие замков» ("Locks opened"), «Окна ПВХ» ("PVC windows"), «Натяжные потолки» ("Stretch ceilings").
  - A debtors' list «Должники за квартплату» ("Rent arrears").

## 10. Courtyard (двор)

- **Layout:** 9-storey slabs and 12–17-storey towers form semi-enclosed yards **≈ 60–120 m across**. Parallel 9-storey blocks stand about 50–70 m apart.
  - The school and kindergarten sit mid-block, inside the microdistrict.
  - Driveways are 3.5–6 m wide; asphalt footpaths 1.5–2.5 m.
  - Kerbs are 0.15 × 0.15 m concrete (1 voxel high), painted white or black-and-white in Luzhkov-era style.
  - Cars park on the pavement: Lada 2107 and 2114, Daewoo Nexia, Renault Logan, Ford Focus.
- **Lighting:** 8–10 m concrete poles with "cobra" heads and **sodium lamps** `#FF9A3C`; about one in five is out. There is also a bulb over each entrance.
- **Playground:** a mix of chipped, rusty Soviet steel and new Luzhkov-era plastic kits. Soviet paint layers: red `#C8312B`, yellow `#E8B92F`, blue `#2F5FA8`, green `#3E8A43`; rust `#8A4B2A`.

| Item | Dimensions and details |
|---|---|
| **Rocket slide** | 5–6 m tall, a 1.5 m-diameter rod cage, cone nose, internal ladder, platform at 2.5–3 m, 4 m chute |
| **Climbing dome** ("globe", "spiderweb") | 3 m diameter, ≈ 1.8 m tall, pipe grid ≈ 0.5 m |
| **Carousel** | 2 m steel disc 0.3 m off the ground, 4–6 handlebars, grinding squeal |
| **Swings** | 2.2–2.5 m portal frame. Seat hangs on **rigid rods** (a full 360° loop is possible). Pendulum period ≈ 3.0 s, rhythmic metal creak |
| **Horizontal bars** | 3 bars at 1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5 m; "Swedish wall" ladders |
| **Seesaw** | 3 m beam |
| **Sandbox** | 2 × 2 m box with a 0.25–0.3 m rim, snow-filled. A **fly-agaric "mushroom" canopy**: a 2.2 m post with a 1.6 m red cap `#B8262A` with white dots |

- **Carpet-beating rack (выбивалка):** two pipe posts 2.0–2.2 m tall and 3 m apart, with a top bar and a bar at 1.2 m. Winter ritual: carpets laid **face-down on fresh snow** and beaten with a rattan beater, leaving grey rectangles. Old clothesline posts with wire are also around.
- **Tyre art:** car tyres (≈ 0.6 m) half-buried as borders and painted in alternating colours. Tyres cut into zig-zag planters and **swans** (white with a red beak).
- **Benches:** by the entrances, 1.5–2 m long, seat at 0.45 m, concrete legs, wooden slats painted green `#3B6B3A`.
- **Bins:** 3–5 steel containers (≈ 1.2 × 0.8 × 1.0 m, 0.75 m³), painted green `#3C6E47` with stencils «ДЕЗ» or «Не бросать мусор мимо!» ("Don't throw rubbish next to the bins!"). They stand in a three-sided brick or sheet enclosure 1.2–1.5 m high, with an 8 m³ skip for bulky waste. **Discarded New Year trees with tinsel** pile up there from 1 January to mid-January.
- **Garages:**
  - **Shell garages (ракушки):** pressed steel clamshells ≈ 5 × 2.6 × 1.8 m, silver, grey or olive, with a front padlock and graffiti «ПРОДАЮ» plus a phone number. Some carry demolition notices «Владелец! Освободите территорию» ("Owner! Vacate the site"); the Luzhkov-era removal campaign was active in 2008–10.
  - **Box garages (пеналы):** 3 × 6 m with swing doors.
  - Brick garage-cooperative rows.
  - 2008 prices: a shell garage cost ≈ 15–25 тыс. руб.; glazing a balcony ≈ 15–30 тыс.; a steel door ≈ 8–20 тыс.
- **Fences:** lawn hoops 0.4–0.5 m high, painted green or yellow-and-green. School fences are 1.8 m steel bars with points (green `#2F5A3A` or black).
- **Trees:**
  - **Pollarded poplars:** 15–20 m, stubby knuckled crowns, bark `#7E8277`.
  - **Birches:** 12–18 m, bark `#E8E4DC` with black dashes `#2A2622`.
  - Norway and ash-leaf maples, lindens.
  - **Rowan with red berries** `#B3261E`.
  - Blue spruces by the school.
