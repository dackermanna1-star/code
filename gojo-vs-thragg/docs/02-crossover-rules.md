# 02 — Crossover Rules & Fight Logic (binding)

This document decides how the two power systems interact, why the fight
goes the way it does, and what every shot must respect. It was produced by:
- a lead draft and two independent designs, one of them deliberately
  contrarian;
- scoring by three specialist judges: a JJK mechanics auditor, an Invincible
  canon auditor, and a director/brief-compliance officer. The contrarian
  design won as the base, with fixes and grafts from the other two;
- re-checking every claim against the verified dossier
  (`01-research-dossier.md`).

**Labels:**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CANON** | Shown or stated in the source |
| **INFERENCE** | Follows directly from canon mechanics, but the crossover case is never shown |
| **CREATIVE** | A production decision for this matchup, never presented on screen as canon |

---

## 1. Continuity choices

**Gojo: the JJK manga (complete, ch 1–271) is primary.** Four mechanics used
here are manga-only (the anime has adapted only to about ch 181). Each is set
up on screen before it decides anything:
- burnout repair with RCT (ch 226–230)
- the touch exemption being exploited (ch 227)
- Unlimited Void's effect on an elite target (ch 229–230)
- "Shoko: about 1 second incapacitates Sukuna" (ch 229)

Gojo wears his 2018 working look: the black high-collar uniform and the black
blindfold. Pairing that outfit with the Shinjuku-era toolkit is a costume-only
liberty.

**Thragg: the Invincible comics, Grand Regent era (#75–#102), are primary.**
- **Why not the animated series:** it has one Thragg fight (S4E7) and never
  shows him hurt, healing or at his limits (T2-20, T2-27).
- **Later issues (#115–#140)** are cited only as evidence of what the same
  body can endure, never as new powers.
- **Excluded:** the animated-only "pull" punch (T2-14), the animated fur
  shawl (T2-30), Battle Beast's pelt (T1-12, post-#120) and the eye injury
  (T1-13).
- **Look:** close-cropped black hair, black mustache, about 198 cm, dark eyes
  (T1-09, T1-10, T2-31). This overrides the brief's "white hair"; see
  03-character-bible.
- **Costume:** the brief's white-and-crimson Grand Regent uniform. The crimson
  entrance cape is a design choice, and Red tears it away in Seq 3.

## 2. Geography & light (fixed for the whole film)

- **The camera always faces NORTH.** West is screen-left; east is
  screen-right.
- **West:** across a river, about 3 km away, a **lit, populated city**, and
  the sun setting behind it. Its light comes in as a warm rim from
  screen-left.
- **The district** is evacuated and dark.
- **Gojo's perch:** a traffic-light mast on the **west corner** of the
  intersection (x = −7 m), so he stands literally between Thragg and the
  lights.
- **East:** abandoned towers, the west-facing block Red throws Thragg through,
  and the unfinished tower (x ≈ +95 m).
- **Screen direction:** Gojo is screen-left, Thragg screen-right. Thragg
  attacks right to left.
- **Light:**
  - A storm-dark dusk throughout.
  - Seq 4's skyline-wide shockwave **tears the cloud deck**. That is the
    on-screen cause of the shafts of evening light in Seq 7, so there is no
    unmotivated weather change.

## 3. Rulings

### R1 — Thragg carries trace cursed energy. **CREATIVE premise, strongly supported**
- **Evidence:**
  - In JJK, cursed energy comes from negative emotion, and virtually all
    humans leak some (G2-31, G2-28).
  - Even people described as having "none" emit extremely faint amounts.
    True zero is reached only through the physically gifted Heavenly
    Restriction (JJK Modulo ch 23; G2-28).
  - The only canonical aliens, Modulo's Simurians, have it (G2-33).
- **Ruling:** Thragg, who has no Heavenly Restriction, leaks a faint,
  untrained, civilian-level trace. He has **no technique, no reinforcement,
  and no Simple Domain, Domain Amplification or Falling Blossom Emotion**
  (T1-44).
- **Why not zero:**
  - Zero is a paid-for JJK restriction. Granting it would be "immune because
    alien", which brief rule 5 forbids.
  - Gojo knows from Toji (who walked unnoticed through Jujutsu High's barrier,
    G2-29) that closed barriers ignore zero-energy beings. Casting Unlimited
    Void on one would be an unforced error.
- **On screen:** in Seq 1 the Six Eyes read "a civilian-level haze wrapped
  around a body of absurd density."

### R2 — Infinity stops everything Thragg throws, swings or reaches with. **CANON** mechanism
- Infinity divides the remaining distance without end; it is not a wall
  (G1-07).
- Its automatic filter weighs mass, speed, shape and cursed-energy intensity
  (G1-09). A 154 kg Viltrumite limb trips mass and shape even at low speed:
  the Seq 4 slow-hand test is an INFERENCE.
- Canon never shows raw force breaking through, and gives no upper limit
  (G1-15, X1-09).
- **On screen:**
  - The fist decelerates and hangs. There is no bounce and no contact flash.
  - Shrapnel and grit carried at dangerous speed hang in the gap.

### R3 — Shockwaves and harmless stimuli. **INFERENCE/CREATIVE**
- Light, sound, breathable air and drifting dust reach Gojo; he sees, hears
  and breathes (G1-10, X1-05).
- A pressure front or shrapnel moving at dangerous speed is held at the
  boundary, like Jogo's explosions (X1-08). Its side lobes radiate outward
  and wreck the surroundings.
- **Continuity consequence:** ordinary drifting dust *can* settle on Gojo.
  The film never uses "dust doesn't touch him" as a cue. The cue is
  **dangerous-speed fragments hanging** (Seq 2) versus **a fragment cutting
  him** (Seq 7).

### R4 — Contact is one-way and chosen. **CANON**
- Gojo can strike, grip and hold through Infinity (G1-08).
- He can switch Infinity off as bait (G2-11: Hanami).
- He can **raise Infinity's output** to push outward and break a hold
  (X1-14).
- Thragg cannot start contact while Limitless is active.

### R5 — The Six Eyes find Thragg but cannot predict him. **INFERENCE**
- Fighters whose physical ability outclasses their cursed energy are hard
  to read by cursed energy (ch 36; Toji, G1-06, G2-29). Thragg's faint trace
  tells Gojo **where** he is, not **what he intends**, nor **how badly hurt
  he is**.
- Gojo therefore evades blows he reads late, out of a Toji-trained reflex,
  even though Infinity would almost certainly hold them.
- He pulls the blindfold down to his neck to read Thragg's body with his
  eyes (Seq 3). Unlimited Void and Hollow Purple are also always cast with
  the eyes uncovered (X1, ch 15/52).

### R6 — Blue and Red move and hurt Thragg; they do not finish him. **CANON** effect, **CREATIVE** dose
- Limitless physically affected zero-energy Toji (G1-20). Blue pulls; Thragg
  can fight a moderate pull with flight thrust (INFERENCE).
- Red has at least twice Blue's output (G1-21).
- External Red damage is calibrated against Toji (thrown, no broken bones,
  X1-38) and Sukuna (survived point-blank, X1-39):
  - Seq 3: split lip, bruising, torn uniform, cape torn away.
  - Seq 4 (point-blank): two cracked ribs, a chest burn.
- **A Red detonated inside an open wound** (Seq 7) faces no skin barrier and
  kills. That is CREATIVE, from T1-53 (Viltrumite skin can be breached),
  #108 (an internal blast killed a Viltrumite duplicate, low confidence) and
  #141 (heart damage defeats Viltrumite healing).
- Gojo's punches use Blue as they land (X1, ch 231). His bare-handed blows
  redirect and set up; they do not decide.

### R7 — Hollow Purple: the core never touches Thragg. **CANON** mechanism, **CREATIVE** margin
- Purple is an imaginary mass that wipes out what it hits (G1-24). It is
  not automatically lethal (Hanami, Sukuna: G1-26, G2-35).
- It **takes time to charge** (ch 233, G1-29 corrected).
- **What happens:**
  - Thragg's speed takes him out of the core.
  - The sphere's outer shell clips his trailing **left forearm, which is
    torn away at mid-forearm**, the stump cauterised.
  - The displacement wake flays his left flank from armpit to hip and tears
    his left ear.
- **What it rests on:** CREATIVE, inferred from Sukuna's reinforced arms
  being destroyed (G1-27) and Toji's arm being severed (G2-34). No claim is
  made about the core versus Viltrumite flesh.
- **Permanence:** no Viltrumite in canon regrows a limb (T1-47, X1-54), so
  the loss is permanent.

### R8 — Unlimited Void on Thragg

**Mechanism.** The sure-hit floods the target with endless information. They
cannot think or move (G1-31). **CANON**

**Targeting.** Per R1, the barrier registers Thragg and the sure-hit lands.
**CREATIVE premise → CANON rule**

**Exemption.** Anyone in physical contact with Gojo is exempt for as long as
the contact lasts, whether or not Gojo wants it (G1-32). **CANON**
- The exemption pauses new information. It does not erase what has already
  arrived. **INFERENCE**

**Duration.**
- Gojo pre-sets about **2.0 s** of real time as part of his split-second
  decision. He has set a domain's length in advance before (Shibuya,
  0.2 s, G1-33).
- 2.0 s is calibrated as double what Shoko says incapacitates Sukuna (about
  1 s, ch 229). **CANON calibration, CREATIVE figure**

**Inside, Gojo may use any technique (no invented restriction).**

Inside, he goes for the heart by hand, through the flayed flank, for
reasons shown on screen:
- His canonical Unlimited Void pattern is to finish by hand: Jogo's head,
  Sukuna's heart (G1-31, G1-35).
- Purple needs charge time inside a 2-second window, as Seq 5 established.
- He has never seen Purple's core touch this body.

**After-effect on Thragg.** **CREATIVE**, never presented as canon. Thragg
regains function within seconds, *degraded*: lag, tremor, unequal pupils,
blood from the nose, ears and tear ducts, and a misjudged first strike.

Grounds:
- The author's comment on ch 229: Unlimited Void probably works less well on
  a curse than on a human, **because their brains are built differently**.
- The Viltrumite brain is demonstrably different: it survives weeks without
  oxygen (#76).
- Sukuna kept fighting after under 10 s of total exposure, at a lasting cost
  (G1-35).
- Thragg fought on disembowelled (T1-26–28) and while burning in the Sun
  (T1-39).

This is will, not recovery. Nothing about his condition resets.

### R9 — Domain burnout. **CANON** (manga), with a stated inference

**What burns out.**
- When the domain ends, Gojo's technique burns out (G1-37, X1-27).
- Teleport is unavailable (ch 226, CANON).
- So are Infinity, Blue and Red (INFERENCE: they are all Limitless).
- Gojo cannot hover during burnout. This is a CREATIVE choice; canon is
  silent (G1-44).

**On screen.** He drops to the asphalt and his shoes skid. Nothing hangs in
the air near him.

**The repair.** Gojo's fix is to damage the part of his brain where the
technique is engraved and heal it with RCT (G1-38, X1-29).
- It costs cumulative brain damage. In Shinjuku, five repairs left him
  unable to open a sixth domain, with a heavy nosebleed.
- He **cannot repair the technique and heal his body at the same time**
  (ch 226).
- Shown as about **1 s** of real time once begun. CREATIVE, approximating
  canon's "at once".

**Gojo's choice ("Shibuya mode").** After the domain he does **not** repair
immediately.

His canon precedent: after the 0.2 s domain in Shibuya he finished his
targets **by hand during burnout** (G1-33). Here he weighs:
- a cheap physical finish against a target that looks frozen, one-armed and
  lung-ruptured, against
- a repair that compounds the brain damage that ended Shinjuku.

He knows strong beings "could wake at any moment" (G2-23), so he moves with
purpose, not leisure. It is a calculated gamble driven by his canonical
arrogance (G2-40). It is not incompetence.

### R10 — RCT (Gojo) vs healing (Thragg). **CANON**

**Gojo**
- RCT heals major trauma (G1-40).
- It slows as his output drops (G1-41).
- It did not save him from bisection (G1-42).
- RCT users die if the brain is destroyed in one blow; Gojo tells Toji as
  much (ch 74).

**Thragg**
- Viltrumite healing is fast but **not** instant (T1-46).
- **No regrowth of lost parts** (T1-47).
- **Heart damage defeats it** (#141).

**Theme: Gojo's losses regrow; Thragg's don't.**

### R11 — Teleport. **CANON**
- It works by compressing space with Limitless (G1-43, "under certain
  conditions"), and only while Limitless is active.
- Used **once**: Seq 6, to intercept Thragg's break for the city.

### R12 — Not used, by rule
- **Black Flash:** not even Gojo can trigger it at will (G1-46).
- **Thragg's sonic/inner-ear weakness as a weapon** (T1-52): nobody in the
  fight produces it. The torn ear only makes his flight list (flavour).
- **The Sun-heat weakness** (T1-54).
- **The animated "pull" punch** (T2-14).

## 4. Winner logic

**Gojo wins. Thragg dies.** A Red detonated inside the chest wound that
Hollow Purple opened destroys his heart, which Viltrumite healing cannot
survive (#141). Gojo loses his left forearm, his first wound in the film.
His RCT will slowly regrow it; Thragg's losses never come back.

There are exactly two doors through Infinity for a being like Thragg, and
canon opens both only from Gojo's side:

1. **Door one: Gojo switches Infinity off on purpose (Seq 4).**
   - After Seq 3, Thragg learns contact is one-way and switches to
     hit-and-run, never staying in reach. Gojo can't fix him in place for a
     point-blank Red.
   - So he drops Infinity as bait (the Hanami precedent). Thragg commits:
     his grip crushes Gojo's collarbone and his spear-hand drives at the
     heart (T1-35).
   - Gojo re-raises Infinity at high output: the grip is pried open and the
     strike decelerates centimetres from his chest. Then a point-blank Red.
   - Thragg watches the collarbone knit with RCT. His line: "So. It is a
     choice." He learns two things: the barrier can be off, and he heals, so
     damage must be total. That is why his last strike goes for the head.
2. **Door two: burnout (Seq 7).**
   - Gojo's sure-hit (Seq 6) nearly finishes him. The touch exemption during
     Gojo's heart strike lets Thragg's body slip the heart off the line,
     costing him a lung instead (CREATIVE reflex; Nolan's slip in T1-34 shows
     a Viltrumite can do it).
   - Gojo gambles on finishing by hand (R9).
   - Thragg, degraded but unbroken (R8), lunges. The lunge misses, but the
     **shard it throws up cuts Gojo's cheek**: the barrier is off.
   - He tears off Gojo's parrying forearm (the Oliver precedent, T1-15) and
     drives a straight right at the head.
   - Gojo's repair completes in the last second. Infinity returns, and the
     fist decelerates 1 cm from his eye, mirroring Seq 2.
   - Two fingers go into the open flank: Red.

**Why this is earned (counterfactual):** remove any one of the following and
Thragg's fist lands on Gojo's skull while he is burned out. Thragg wins, or
both die.
- the Purple flank wound
- the domain lag
- Gojo's choice to save the repair
- the forearm parry that bought the last second

Knowledge decides it:
- Thragg reads "barrier off" correctly, from evidence.
- He cannot know that Gojo can repair his own brain.
- A fight that ends in the domain of infinite information is decided by who
  knows what.

## 7. Logged deviations from the brief (for sign-off)

1. **Thragg's hair.** Canon (close-cropped black hair, black mustache)
   replaces the brief's "thick swept-back white hair". Canon accuracy is the
   brief's own first rule. Two white heads would also merge in dusk wides.
2. **The domain hand sign.** Canon is one hand, middle finger crossed over
   the index (G1-30). The brief says "brings his hands together".
3. **A lit city across the river.** It is an addition to the "abandoned
   district", which is kept. It creates the stakes and Thragg's canonical
   hostage tactic (T1-19). No people are ever shown.
4. **Seq 7's final strike.** Thragg's fist goes to the head, as the brief
   wants ("Thragg's fist driving forward"), rather than his signature heart
   spear-hand. The spear-hand is used, and fails, in Seq 4. The change of
   target is something he learns on screen.
5. **Time scales.**
   - **Unlimited Void:** about 2.0 s of real time is shown as about 20 s of
     perception time. The domain is, by definition, a flood of experienced
     time, and Gojo's perception is canonically accelerated (Prison Realm,
     G2-16).
   - **Seq 7, before the slow motion:** about 6 s of real action spread over
     about 16 s of screen. It plays near real time while Gojo walks in and
     Thragg struggles, then as overlapping angles for the final 1.2 s
     exchange, which the brief asks for ("extremely fast alternating
     close-ups").
   - **The only true slow motion** is the Seq 7 intersection beat.
