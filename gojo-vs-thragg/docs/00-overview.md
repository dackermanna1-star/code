# 00 — Overview

**THE STRONGEST SORCERER VS. THE KING OF VILTRUMITES**
Satoru Gojo vs. Grand Regent Thragg · exactly 240 s · 16:9 · 24 fps · 4K target

## The fight in one paragraph

An evacuated district at storm-dark dusk. Across the river, a lit city.
Grand Regent Thragg lands in the empty intersection. Satoru Gojo is
standing on a traffic-light mast that happens to sit between Thragg and the
lights.
- **Infinity holds.** Every blow Thragg throws decelerates without end and
  hangs short of Gojo, whatever its speed, mass or angle. Thragg stops
  attacking and starts *investigating*.
- **The first door.** Thragg learns that contact is one-way, so Gojo has to
  drop Infinity on purpose to bait him in. Thragg's grip breaks Gojo's
  collarbone before Infinity pries it open. Then Thragg watches the bone
  heal: "So. It is a choice."
- **Hollow Purple misses, barely.** Thragg uses the lit city as a shield.
  The beat Gojo spends finding a safe angle lets Thragg's speed slip the
  core, at the cost of his left forearm.
- **The domain.** Thragg breaks for the city. Gojo teleports into his path
  and opens Unlimited Void for exactly two seconds. The touch exemption, the
  rule that anyone in contact with Gojo is unaffected, lets Thragg's body
  slip Gojo's heart strike, and he loses a lung instead of his life.
- **Burnout.** Gojo's technique burns out when the domain ends. He gambles
  on finishing by hand. A shard from Thragg's lunge cuts his cheek, and
  Thragg *reads* it: the barrier is off. He tears off Gojo's parrying
  forearm and drives a fist at his head.
- **The last centimetre.** Gojo's brain repair finishes in the final
  second. The fist stops 1 cm from his eye, and a Red detonates inside the
  wound that Purple opened.
- **The end.** Thragg dies in the crater beneath the toppled mast. Gojo
  stands one-armed in front of the untouched city and pulls his blindfold
  back on: "Told you."

## Why Gojo wins, and why it's close

- **Thragg never breaks through Infinity.** Raw force never has, in canon.
  He gets to Gojo only through the two doors canon allows, both opened from
  Gojo's side: Gojo switching Infinity off, and domain burnout.
- **Both doors nearly kill Gojo.**
- **Knowledge decides it.** Thragg correctly reads that the barrier is off.
  He cannot know that Gojo can repair his own brain.
- **Gojo's losses heal; Thragg's never come back.**
