# Original Brief (Phase 1 verbatim; Phase 3 near-verbatim; Phases 2 and 4–9 condensed without dropping requirements)

PROJECT TITLE: THE STRONGEST SORCERER VS. THE KING OF VILTRUMITES
SATORU GOJO VS. GRAND REGENT THRAGG
DURATION: EXACTLY 4 MINUTES (240 SECONDS)
GENRE: High-budget cinematic anime action, brutal superhero combat, supernatural warfare, cosmic-scale destruction
FORMAT: 16:9 widescreen, 4K target resolution, 24 FPS cinematic presentation, with fluid 2D/3D hybrid animation
VISUAL QUALITY: Theatrical anime movie quality, exceptional character animation, physically convincing destruction, sophisticated visual effects, detailed compositing, and professional sound design
TONE: Intense, merciless, suspenseful, visually spectacular, intelligent, and emotionally gripping
CORE OBJECTIVE: Create an unforgettable, expertly choreographed four-minute battle between Satoru Gojo from Jujutsu Kaisen and Grand Regent Thragg from Invincible. Make every second visually purposeful. The battle must feel like a clash between two fundamentally different forms of overwhelming power: an untouchable master of space and cursed energy versus an extraordinarily powerful Viltrumite warrior.
Do not create a slideshow, a collection of disconnected attacks, or a repetitive sequence of characters punching each other. Create a coherent, cinematic fight with tactical adaptation, readable action, distinct fighting styles, escalating stakes, stunning animation, and a satisfying conclusion.

## PHASE 1: MANDATORY CHARACTER RESEARCH
Before generating any scenes, thoroughly research both characters using reliable primary or official sources wherever possible.
Research their canonical abilities, limitations, personalities, fighting styles, physical characteristics, signature attacks, and demonstrated combat feats.
Use the original Jujutsu Kaisen manga and anime for Satoru Gojo and the Invincible comics and animated series for Thragg. Distinguish between comic and animated continuity when their feats or portrayals differ.
Do not invent abilities, confuse characters with other characters, or give either fighter powers they have never demonstrated without clearly treating them as a creative adaptation.

### A. SATORU GOJO — CHARACTER ACCURACY
Research and accurately portray:
* Six Eyes: Gojo's extraordinary perception and precise manipulation of cursed energy.
* Limitless: His inherited technique for manipulating space.
* Infinity: The defensive application that prevents conventional attacks from reaching him by effectively dividing the space between Gojo and an approaching object.
* Blue: The attractive force produced by the Limitless technique.
* Red: The repulsive force produced by reversing the Limitless technique.
* Hollow Purple: The devastating combination of Blue and Red.
* Unlimited Void: Gojo's Domain Expansion, which overwhelms its target with immense informational stimulation.
* Reverse Cursed Technique: His ability to use positive energy to heal injuries.
* Teleportation and high-speed movement: Portray these according to established conditions and limitations rather than as unlimited, consequence-free abilities.
* Hand-to-hand combat: Gojo is an exceptional close-quarters fighter, not merely a stationary character who fires energy attacks.
* Combat intelligence: He can analyze opponents, improvise, exploit openings, and change tactics during a fight.

Gojo's visual identity — Maintain his recognizable appearance:
* Tall, lean, athletic build.
* Distinctive white hair with its characteristic spiky silhouette.
* Black blindfold or dark sunglasses, depending on the chosen scene and canonical presentation.
* Black high-collar uniform.
* Piercing blue eyes when revealed.
* Relaxed confidence, playful arrogance, and an unsettling sense of control.

His cursed energy and techniques should look sophisticated rather than like generic blue magic.
Visual language:
* Infinity: Subtle spatial distortion, refracted scenery, suspended dust, and attacks that slow progressively as they approach him.
* Blue: Concentrated blue energy, environmental attraction, imploding debris, and directional gravitational distortion.
* Red: A brilliant crimson release with powerful outward shockwaves.
* Hollow Purple: A visually distinctive fusion of Blue and Red, rendered as a catastrophic spatial-energy attack with a clear origin, trajectory, and impact.
* Unlimited Void: An overwhelming domain transition with a surreal, information-saturated visual environment, inspired by the canonical presentation.

Do not portray Infinity as a conventional glowing force field that simply absorbs damage. Its defining feature is that ordinary attacks cannot straightforwardly reach Gojo.
Do not make Gojo invulnerable to every conceivable ability. Show the difference between conventional physical attacks, which Infinity can stop, and any genuinely established mechanism that might interact with his technique.

### B. GRAND REGENT THRAGG — CHARACTER ACCURACY
Research Thragg's strongest canonical feats and distinguish between his comic-book portrayal and his animated-series portrayal.
Use a consistent continuity for the fight. If the animated series has not yet established a feat, do not falsely present it as an animated-series feat. If the battle draws on the comics, establish a comics-informed interpretation of Thragg's abilities.
Accurately portray:
* Viltrumite superhuman strength: Extraordinary striking power, grappling ability, and destructive physical force.
* Extreme speed: Rapid acceleration, high-speed flight, and devastating movement through combat.
* Flight: Powerful aerial maneuverability and the ability to change direction at tremendous speed.
* Exceptional durability: His ability to endure extraordinary physical punishment.
* Combat endurance: His capacity to continue fighting through injuries and exhaustion.
* Advanced hand-to-hand combat: Efficient, ruthless, disciplined, and experienced fighting technique.
* Tactical intelligence: His ability to identify an opponent's weaknesses and adapt instead of blindly repeating attacks.
* Battlefield destruction: His physical attacks should produce consequences appropriate to his demonstrated power.
* Relentlessness: Thragg fights to win, using efficient violence rather than theatrical gestures that expose him unnecessarily.

Thragg's visual identity — Maintain his recognizable appearance:
* Extremely muscular, broad-shouldered, imposing physique.
* Pale skin and characteristic facial structure.
* Thick, swept-back white hair.
* Distinctive Viltrumite mustache.
* White-and-red Viltrumite uniform.
* A cold, intimidating expression.
* Economical movements that communicate confidence, experience, and lethal intent.

Thragg must look physically powerful even when standing still. His posture, eye contact, controlled breathing, and minimal movements should communicate that he is a veteran conqueror who has fought countless dangerous opponents.
Visual language:
* Supersonic acceleration communicated through pressure waves, air displacement, environmental deformation, and sonic booms where appropriate.
* Punches that create powerful shockwaves and visibly transfer momentum into their targets or surroundings.
* Flight paths that tear through clouds and displace debris.
* Heavy impacts with convincing mass, follow-through, and recovery.
* Injuries that accumulate consistently rather than disappearing between shots.

Avoid making Thragg behave like a generic screaming brute. His defining quality is not simply strength; it is the intelligence and ruthlessness with which he uses that strength.

### C. POWER-SCALING AND CANONICAL LOGIC
This battle must respect the distinction between physical power and spatial or supernatural abilities.
Thragg's raw strength, speed, durability, and combat experience should make him an extraordinary physical threat. However, do not automatically assume that being faster or stronger allows him to bypass Infinity.
Likewise, do not automatically assume that Gojo's techniques will instantly defeat Thragg.
Apply the following rules:
1. Ordinary physical attacks must interact with Infinity according to Gojo's established mechanics.
2. Thragg must recognize that his usual attacks are not working and investigate the reason.
3. Gojo must recognize that Thragg's speed, physical power, and combat instincts make him dangerous even when direct attacks are ineffective.
4. Blue, Red, Hollow Purple, and Unlimited Void must behave consistently with their established mechanics.
5. Do not assume that Thragg is immune to cursed techniques simply because he is an alien, or that he automatically possesses cursed energy.
6. Do not assume that Domain Expansion automatically guarantees victory against every opponent regardless of the mechanics involved.
7. If Unlimited Void affects Thragg, show the mechanism and consequences clearly. If his resistance is being interpreted creatively, do not present that resistance as established canon.
8. Do not introduce arbitrary weaknesses, convenient power-ups, unexplained immunities, or sudden power increases just to force a predetermined result.
9. Preserve consistent injury, stamina, technique usage, positioning, and environmental damage.
10. Every major tactical change must follow from something the characters observe or experience.

If canonical evidence cannot establish a definitive interaction between two different fictional power systems, acknowledge the uncertainty internally and make a consistent creative decision for this particular matchup.
The goal is not to make one character artificially superior. The goal is to create a battle in which their different abilities produce intelligent, spectacular, and consequential interactions.

## PHASE 3: EXACT FOUR-MINUTE STORYBOARD (strict timeline)
SEQUENCE 1 — THE ENCOUNTER 00:00–00:25 (25 s). Establish atmosphere, character, tension. Abandoned metropolitan district at dusk, storm-darkened sky, wind through shattered windows, dust across an empty intersection, distant thunder. Gojo does not move; looks at the fist, then at Thragg. Gojo: "That's close. For you." Thragg follows: straight, hook, knee, rapid combination at different body parts; each fails. ## PHASE 4: COMBAT CHOREOGRAPHY RULES
For every major attack: (1) what the attacker is trying to accomplish; (2) how the defender recognizes/reacts; (3) why it succeeds, fails, or changes the battlefield; (4) what the attacker learns; (5) how the next exchange develops from it. No repetitive alternation without learning. Distinct styles — Gojo: effortless confidence, spatial manipulation, precise efficient movement, deliberate positioning, unpredictable counters, techniques integrated into hand-to-hand, calm observation → serious concentration. Thragg: ruthless aggression, exceptional physical power, high-speed flight/acceleration, precise punishing strikes, tactical feints, grappling and environmental destruction, continuous adaptation. Neither stands still firing attacks. Impact quality: anticipation frames, strong contact poses, directional motion blur, brief camera shake, air displacement, debris away from impact, ground deformation, recoil, recovery, scale-appropriate sound; light strike / heavy punch / cursed-energy blast / city-scale impact each have different visual and audio signatures. Damage continuity: injuries persist until a canonical healing mechanism; RCT use portrayed consistently; torn clothing, blood, bruising, breathing/fatigue, environmental damage, destroyed buildings, lighting/weather, relative positions; never reset appearance.
