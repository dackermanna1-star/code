## Sequence 2 — The First Impact  ·  00:25:00–00:55:00 (30 s)

**Objective:** Demonstrate Thragg's terrifying physical power and introduce Infinity as deceleration, not a wall; Thragg stops attacking and starts investigating.  
**Tactical logic:** Thragg's opening move is to end the fight with one blow (S2-01). It slows without end 1 cm from Gojo's blindfold, and grit thrown at bullet speed hangs in the gap. This is the plant Seq 7 pays off. From then on every attack is an experiment that rules out one hypothesis. Target and trajectory: a hook to the temple, a knee to the ribs, a low kick to the knee (S2-04 to S2-06). Footing: the sweep shears the mast arm, and Gojo keeps standing on nothing (S2-06, S2-07). Reflex: a feint gets no reaction, and the real strike comes from the blind flank, then from directly above (S2-08, S2-09). Thragg's eyes go from confusion to analysis: he isn't defending, something is (S2-10). Mass: a four-tonne slab stalls and then simply drops (S2-11). Speed and attention: a 300 m loop through the storm deck, then a maximum-speed dive from high behind Gojo's right (S2-12, S2-13). Gojo turns calmly to face him before he arrives. The stall is identical, and the boom wrecks the street around him but not his collar (S2-14). Thragg's conclusion is that this is a property of the space around the man, so conventional force is not enough. He withdraws to 30 m and studies Gojo with growing hostility (S2-15), which hands off to Seq 3's Blue. Gojo never defends, never moves a hand to block, and never evades: Infinity is automatic (G1-09, X1-03). His only actions are acting beats: a head tilt, a weight shift, a pinch gesture and the turn. Screen direction stays Gojo-left/Thragg-right throughout. The one reversal (S2-13) is set up by the visible contrail loop in S2-12 and restored by Thragg's backward withdrawal in S2-15.
